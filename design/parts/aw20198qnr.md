## Current setting, R35 = 47 kΩ 1 % (C25792, basic, already on the BOM for B)

The current formula is ILED = (400 V / REXT) × (GCC/255) × (SL/255) × (PWM/256) × DUTY (p.18).

- **Peak:** Imax = 400 / 47 k = **8.51 mA** per die. That's B's 8.09 mA, within 5 %, so the firmware's levels carry over.
- **Duty:** SWSEL = 1000 scans SW1–SW9 (p.24 GCR). Each slot is 0.25 + 5 × 0.125 + 16 + 0.125 + 1 = 18.0 µs, with 15.94 µs
  of PWM. So DUTY = 15.94 / 18.0 / 9 = **0.0984**. That checks against p.18's own 11-SW example, 0.0805.
- **Average at full scale:** 8.51 × 0.0984 = **0.837 mA** per die.
- **Full white, hardware cap:** 135 dies × 0.837 = **113 mA**, or ~128 mA with the rest of the system. That's under the
  cell's 140 mA, even with a firmware bug. It's B's argument for 47 k, and it still holds.
- **The red trade-off.** The XL-1615's red is weak at low current (see `xl-1615rgbc.md`). So **dim with PWM and keep SL
  and GCC high**: the instantaneous current then stays near 8.5 mA, where red still works. Dimming with GCC or SL instead
  lowers the instantaneous current itself.
  - A lower REXT would buy red headroom: 39 k gives 10.3 mA peak and 136 mA full white. That is over the cell's budget once
    the MCU is counted, so this audit keeps 47 k. The user can trade this.
- **Recommended config:** p.16 gives GCC 0x05–0x80 and PWM ≥ 0x20 at PVCC 4.2 V. That is for open/short detection
  accuracy only, not a limit.

## Headroom (LED lights only if VSYS ≥ VF + VHR_SW + VHR_CS)

- **Headroom ratings:** VHR_CS is 300 mV at 40 mA and VHR_SW is 750 mV at 720 mA (p.7). At 8.5 mA and 128 mA they scale
  to roughly 0.07 V and 0.13 V.
- **VF:** green and blue are ~2.75 V at 8.5 mA (XL-1615 p.6 curve), with the 3.4 V max bin at 20 mA.
- **Floor:** a straight scaling gives G/B a floor of VSYS ≥ ~2.95 V. The review (m1) points out that sink compliance does
  not scale linearly: the real floor is ≈ 3.05 V typical, and 3.25–3.4 V for top-VF bins. So the **firmware's
  low-battery colour floor should be ≥ 3.3 V**. The SW bus's IR drop eats into this too, which makes it a gate 7 check.

## Supply and power

- **Quiescent:** **1.8 mA** active with all LEDs off (p.7), against the IS31FL3741A's 3.6 mA. That's ~1.8 mA off every
  awake mode in variant B's battery table.
- **Standby:** 1.3 µA with EN low or CHIPEN = 0, registers kept. The 1 MΩ EN pull-down holds it there when the MCU is
  off. VSYS is not cut by the soft latch, so this is the earring's "off" draw from this part: >10 years from 140 mAh.
  The IS31 needed an external 100 k pull-down (R36). This one doesn't, so R36 is gone.
- **Low-power mode:** MIXCR.LPEN = 1 drops into low-power mode automatically whenever every PWM is 0 (p.10–11). Firmware
  should set it.
- **Thermal, worst case:** full white on USB, VSYS 4.95 V.
  - Dissipation is 0.837 mA × (45 × (4.95 − 1.95) + 90 × (4.95 − 2.75)) = 279 mW, plus 9 mW quiescent.
  - At θJA 33 °C/W that is **+9.5 °C**. The board is smaller than JEDEC's, so call it +20 °C. Fine.

  has no job.
- **Layout (p.35):** caps and REXT as close as possible. The EP needs as many thermal vias as possible. That is a gate 7
  item, as in B, and via-in-pad is the user's call.

## De-ghost and LED reverse voltage (p.20–21)

- **How it works:** the CS pull-ups (0.5–32 kΩ) and SW pull-downs (0.25–16 kΩ) are selected in DGCR. They act during the
  off/blanking time.
  With the default PUMD = PDMD = 0 they act only then.
- **Reverse bias:** an unlit LED can see up to ~VSYS reverse: 4.95 V on USB against the XL-1615's 5 V VR. That's inside
  the rating but thin, and limited to µA-level current by the pull resistors. The same holds for the IS31 in B.
- **Firmware:** CSPUR = 000 (CS pull-up off) removes the reverse bias entirely. Otherwise, pick the largest DGCR resistances
  that still kill ghosting.
- **Actual USB VSYS:** D3 is a 1N4007WS silicon diode, so the real figure is below the 4.95 V assumed here. That figure is
  kept as the worst case.

## Firmware notes (review, 2026-11-05)

- **Start-up:** wait 2 ms after power-on and 100 µs after EN before the first I²C write. UVLO and over-temperature both
  clear CHIPEN, so re-enable it after either.
- **Logic level:** GCR.LGC = 1 (2.4 / 0.6 V) gives the 3.3 V I/O more noise margin than the default.
- **Audible noise:** the scan-frame current envelope (~6.2 kHz) sits next to the ear and can make MLCCs sing. Enable spread
  spectrum and slew-rate control.
- **Register indexing:** the LED register index runs SWn × 18 + CS. CS16–18 are unused, so the index skips them.
