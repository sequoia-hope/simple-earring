## Calculations

### Matrix shape: 9 SW × 15 CS (recommended) against 5 SW × 27 CS

Duty is 32 µs on in each 36 µs slot (tSCAN 32 + tNOL1 2 + tNOL2 2, p.17, Formula 2), times 1/n for n scanned SW
lines.
- For n = 9 the datasheet gives ILED = IOUT/10.125.
- For SWS = 0100 (n = 5) it does not print the duty. Table 11 (p.14) and Formula 2 imply 32/36 × 1/5, so ILED =
  IOUT/5.625. Assumed; measure at bring-up.

Both columns use RISET chosen for the same full-scale average (~0.8 mA per die):

| | 9 SW × 15 CS, RISET 47 k | 5 SW × 27 CS, RISET 82 k |
|---|---|---|
| IOUT per die at GCC = SL = 255 (×(255/256)²) | 383/47k × 0.992 = **8.09 mA** | 383/82k × 0.992 = **4.63 mA** |
| ILED average at PWM 255 | 8.09/10.125 = **0.80 mA** | 4.63/5.625 = **0.82 mA** |
| Dies on one SW | 15 | 27 |
| Peak SW (supply) current, all on | 15 × 8.09 = **121 mA** | 27 × 4.63 = **125 mA** |
| SW headroom at that current (VHR 550 typ / 750 max mV at 800 mA, taken as resistive) | 83 / 114 mV | 86 / 117 mV |
| G/B Vf at IOUT (p.6 V-I curve, typ) | ~2.75 V | ~2.65 V |
| CS lines (bus round the ring) | 15: 3.8 mm at 0.254 mm pitch, 1.9 mm per layer on In2 + B.Cu | 27: 6.9 mm, 3.4 mm per layer |
| Driver pins used | SW1–9 (pins 16–24) + CS1–15 (pins 26–35, 37–41): **two adjacent sides** (bottom, right) | CS1–27 runs over the bottom, right and top sides |
| I²C frame (135 PWM bytes) | 9 row writes + 2 page changes, ~3.9 ms at 400 kHz | one page, one burst, ~3.4 ms |
| Growth with no hardware change | +3 CS per 9 LEDs, up to 117 RGB | up to 65 RGB, then a re-map to more SW |

**Peak supply current is the same for any shape.** The current per SW slot is (135/n) × IOUT. IOUT = n × 1.125 × ILED,
so the slot current is 135 × 1.125 × ILED, independent of n. "5 SW halves the peak" (variant-b-matrix.md) is true
per die (8.1 mA against 4.6 mA), not for the supply. Both per-die peaks are far under the LED's 20 mA continuous limit,
so the halving buys nothing.

**Ghosting** comes out about even. Every CS line runs round the whole ring in both shapes, so line capacitance is similar,
and the slot rate (27.8 kHz) does not depend on n. Ghost charge relative to lit charge goes as C·ΔV·f/ILED, with no n
in it. The de-ghost pulls handle either (fine print).

**Headroom:** 5 × 27 needs ~0.1 V less VBATT for blue and green, at the hardware maximum only.

**Recommendation: 9 SW × 15 CS at 1/9 duty.** The 15-line bus is about half as wide, all matrix pins sit on two sides of
the QFN facing one direction, the board can grow to 117 LEDs, and the supply peak is the same. 5 × 27 wins only ~0.1 V of
headroom at a brightness the firmware never uses.

### RISET = 47 kΩ

What the dim range needs, matched in luminance to today's WS2816C (90 / 190 / 25 mcd R/G/B at ~3.5 mA per channel,
WS2816C ds) at the firmware's levels:
- **Show (drip):** `GLOBAL_BRIGHTNESS = 8` (main.cpp:94) through NeoPixel's (b+1)>>8 scaler, HSV hue 49152 (purple: R 127, B 255 before the scaler).
  The brightest frame is R 4 and B 8 counts → R 1.41 mcd, B 0.78 mcd.
- **Charge indicator:** `CHG_LEVEL = 10` raw counts (main.cpp:148): R up to 10 counts → 3.5 mcd, G up to 3 → 2.2 mcd.
- **XL-1010 efficiency at 20 mA** (xl-1010rgb.md): R 5–10, G 27.5–50, B 4–10 mcd/mA, bin min–max.
- **Average current needed per die:**
  - show R 0.14–0.28 mA and B 0.08–0.20 mA;
  - charge R 0.35–0.71 mA and G 0.05–0.08 mA.

  **The brightest need is about 0.71 mA** (charge red, lowest bin).

RISET sets the full scale, PWM 255 × SL 255 × GCC 255. Pick it so that full scale sits just above that need and full
white stays inside the cell's 140 mA continuous rating with the rest of the system on:

| RISET | IOUT | ILED full scale | Full white, typ (+8 % mismatch) | + ~15 mA system (MCU 10, IMU 0.5, ICC 3.6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 39 k | 9.74 mA | 0.96 mA | 130 (140) mA | 145 mA: over 140 |
| 43 k | 8.84 mA | 0.87 mA | 118 (127) mA | 133 (142) mA: at the edge |
| **47 k** | **8.09 mA** | **0.80 mA** | **108 (116) mA** | **123 (131) mA: inside** |

With 47 k:
- **PWM use.** At GCC = 255 the brightest need (0.71 mA) lands at PWM ≈ 227 for lowest-bin red, and ≈ 150 for a typical
  bin. Show mode runs at GCC ≈ 100 to put its brightest purple near PWM 230.
- **Colour balance.** Per-LED Scaling does it: green needs only ~1/5 of red's current for the same weight (SL_G ≈ 50).
- **Hard cap.** The hardware cannot exceed the cell rating even if firmware writes 0xFF everywhere.
- **Trade.** The 8 % IOUT spread (34.96–41.04 mA at 10 k, p.5) is specified only at RISET = 10 kΩ, so matching at 47 k
  (ISET current ~4.7× lower) is not guaranteed. Per-LED SL trimming covers it if it matters. An open question, not a
  blocker.

A 10 kΩ fitted by mistake gives 38 mA peaks at 1/10.125 duty. That stays inside the LED's 60/80 mA pulse rating
(≤ 0.1 ms, ≤ 1/10), with an average of 3.75 mA. Not destructive.

### IF peak and average limits (LED abs max, xl-1010rgb.md)

- Peak per die 8.09 mA (8.7 mA at +8 %), in 32 µs pulses at 1/10.125 duty. That is under the 20 mA continuous rating, so
  the pulse rating (R 60, G/B 80 mA) is not needed.
- Average ≤ 0.80 mA (0.86 at +8 %) against 20 mA continuous; the derating curve gives ~10 mA at 85 °C (p.6).
- Power per die at the hardware maximum: G/B 2.75 V × 0.80 mA = 2.2 mW against 70 mW; R 1.95 V × 0.80 = 1.6 mW
  against 50 mW.

### Headroom (9 × 15, 47 k, at the hardware maximum)

VBATT needed for a full-white SW slot = Vf(G/B at 8 mA) + VHR,CS + VHR,SW + path drop.
- **Path drop:** cell IR < 0.2 Ω (bt1-lipo-cell.md) + Q2 CJ2101 ~0.21 Ω, × 121 mA ≈ 50 mV.
- **Typical:** 2.75 + 0.35 + 0.08 + 0.05 = **3.23 V**.
- **Worst:** Vf 3.25 V (top bin N13-5, 3.4 V at 20 mA, less ~0.15 V down the curve to 8 mA) + 0.60 + 0.11 + 0.05 =
  **4.0 V**.
- The CS figure is the 38 mA specification. A sink at 8 mA saturates lower, so 0.35/0.60 V is an upper bound.
- Typical parts regulate over the whole useful cell range: the system stops near 3.4 V anyway.
- A top-bin blue or green at full scale drops out of regulation below ~4.0 V. It dims; nothing is damaged. At realistic
  levels (GCC ≈ 100 → IOUT ≈ 3.2 mA) the requirement falls to ≲ 3.4–3.7 V.
- Firmware should keep SL·GCC low and may raise PWM rather than IOUT as VBATT falls.

### Dissipation and temperature

P(driver) ≈ Σ (VSYS − Vf) × ILED + VSYS × ICC, with Vf R ≈ 1.95 V and G/B ≈ 2.75 V at 8 mA. ICC is 4.0 mA at 5 V and
3.6 mA at 3.6 V (p.5).

| Case | VSYS | LED current | P(driver) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hardware max, full white, USB | 4.95 V | 108 mA (116 at +8 %) | **286 mW (309)** |
| Hardware max, full white, battery | 4.20 V | 108 mA | 202 mW |
| Firmware realistic max: all 45 LEDs at full drip, lowest bin (below) | 4.95 V | ~21 mA | **~73 mW** |
| Same on battery | 4.20 V | ~21 mA | ~55 mW |

- θJA 33.1 °C/W is the JEDEC 4-layer test board (76 × 114 mm, p.5). This board is a 30.5 mm 4-layer disc, so allow 2–3×:
  65–100 °C/W.
  - Hardware max on USB: +20 to +31 °C → TJ ≤ ~71 °C at 40 °C ambient (on a body, under hair).
  - Realistic: +5 to +7 °C. OK.
- **The firmware's realistic maximum:**
  - Drip show, every LED at full intensity (they never all are): R 0.19 + B 0.11 = 0.30 mA per LED typical bin, and 0.48
    mA lowest bin. × 45 = 13.5–21.6 mA.
  - Charge arc: at most 37 LEDs; worst near 50 % charge, ~19 LEDs × (R 5 + G 2 counts) ≈ 5–8 mA.
  - The realistic maximum is therefore ~21 mA of LED current. Cell: 21 + 15 = 36 mA, a quarter of its 140 mA rating.

### Bulk capacitance

- **Edges.** The scan steps the supply by up to 121 mA (hardware max), or ~20 mA realistic, every 36 µs.
- **Holding a slot is not practical.** It would take C = I·t/ΔV = 0.121 × 36 µs / 50 mV = 87 µF. A 22 µF 0603 at 4–5 V is
  ~10 µF effective and would still sag 0.4 V, so round-pendant's 22 µF does not hold the row either.
- **The droop is resistive, and small:** 0.41 Ω × 121 mA ≈ 50 mV on battery. On USB, D3's dynamic resistance (~0.5–1 Ω
  at 0.1 A) gives ~0.1 V. The NCP730 rejects it.
- **What the bulk is for:** keeping the fast edges local to the driver instead of on the VSYS run back to C29.
- **Choice:** one 10 µF 0603 (C19702, the same part as C29). Estimated effective value at 4.2–4.95 V is 4–5 µF, X5R
  DC-bias derating. That is from Samsung's typical curves, not re-read today.

### MLCC derating (rating ≥ 1.5 × 4.95 V = 7.4 V)

|---|---|---|
| C52923 1 µF X5R 0402 | 25 V | ~0.6–0.7 µF |

### I²C pull-ups: 4.7 kΩ, 400 kHz

- **Rise time.** tr(30–70 %) = 0.847 RC. The bus is one device and a ~20 mm run (front MCU, one via, back driver), so
  Cb ≈ 20–30 pF → tr ≈ 80–120 ns.
  - Fast mode allows 300 ns (p.6): passes with 2.5× margin.
  - Fm+ (1 MHz) allows 120 ns: marginal at 30 pF. 1 MHz would need ~2.2 kΩ; the datasheet's 1 kΩ is for long buses.
- **Speed is not needed.** A full frame is ~174 bytes with addressing, 3.9 ms at 400 kHz.
- **Sink current** 3.3 V / 4.7 k = 0.70 mA, so VOL is negligible on both ends.

### I²C logic level at 3V3 (the LGC bit)

- **The LGC = 0 levels have to work.** LGC (PG4 00h D3) powers up 0 and resets to 0 on 3Fh = 0xAE, so the driver receives
  the write that would set it at the default levels: VIL 0.4 V, VIH 1.4 V (p.6).
- **High side:** RP2350 VOH ≥ 2.62 V, and the pull-up takes the line to 3.3 V. Against VIH 1.4 V there is ample margin.
- **Low side.** RP2350 VOL is ≤ 0.5 V at IOL = the drive setting (2/4/8/12 mA; RP2350 ds Table 1684). That is above VIL
  0.4 V if the pad is loaded to its rating. Our pull-up sinks 0.70 mA:
  - in 12 mA drive mode, VOL ≤ 0.5 × 0.70/12 ≈ 0.03 V, taking the pad as resistive in its linear region. That is a
    derivation, not a specification.
  - in the default 4 mA mode, ≈ 0.09 V.
- **Recommendation: keep LGC = 0 and set GPIO16/17 to 8 or 12 mA drive.** This differs from round-pendant, which set
  LGC = 1. LGC = 1 raises VIH to 2.4 V: high-side margin 0.9 V, and a 1.3 RC instead of 0.55 RC wait to cross it. It buys
  low-side margin we already have.
- **Gap:** the driver's own SDA VOL (ACK and reads) is not specified. The RP2350 reads with VIL 0.8 V; check at bring-up.

### CS resistors: not needed

The figure's 51 Ω (red) and 20 Ω (G/B) on CS take the excess (5 V − Vf) drop off the chip at 38 mA per CS. At 8 mA:
- the whole matrix dissipates ≤ 0.31 W at the hardware maximum, and ~70 mW realistic;
- a 51 Ω resistor would drop 51 Ω × 8.1 mA = 0.41 V. At full white that moves only ~0.41 V × 36 mA ≈ 15 mW off the
  chip per colour, while taking 0.41 V of headroom on battery. They cost headroom for no thermal gain.

So: **none**, and no reverse-voltage benefit either (below).

### Reverse voltage on off LEDs at VSYS max

- **During tNOL1 (2 µs per slot)** every SW is off and pulled down, and every CS is off and pulled up (Table 13).
  - Each LED sees V(CS) − V(SW), up to ≈ PVCC = VSYS: **4.95 V against VR 5 V** (LED p.3). That is inside the rating with
    50 mV to spare.
  - The datasheet does not say the CS pull-up goes to PVCC; it is assumed.
  - The 5 V is also the IR ≤ 1 µA test point, not a characterised breakdown.
  - Whatever reverse current flows is limited by the pulls, ≤ 4.95 V / 64 kΩ = 77 µA at 32 kΩ each, for 2 µs in 36.
- **During scanning,** off LEDs sharing a lit CS see at most VSYS − Vf ≈ 2.2–3 V.
- **Mitigations, if bring-up shows reverse leakage or VBUS runs high:**
  - disable the CS pull-ups (PDR = 000) and keep the SW pull-downs;
  - or accept it, since the energy is negligible.
- On battery (≤ 4.20 V) the margin is 0.8 V. On round-pendant (VSYS 4.5 V) it was 0.5 V.

### Quiescent and shutdown currents (battery budget)

| State | Current | Source |
|---|---|---|
| On, PWM = 0 (dark frame) | ICC 3.6 mA typ, 4.5 max at 3.6 V; 4.0 / 5.5 at 5 V | p.5, measured at RISET 10 k; may be a little lower at 47 k (unverified) |
| SDB pull-down while on | 3.3 V / 100 kΩ = 33 µA | R36 |
| I²C pull-ups | 0 idle; ~0.7 mA per line while low, ~5 % bus use → ~0.03 mA average at 30 fps | — |
| Software shutdown (SSD = 0) or SDB low (nap) | 0.5 µA typ, 1 µA max at 3.6 V; 1 / 3 µA at 5 V | p.5; registers retained (p.19) |

So the battery budget is ~3.65 mA while showing (driver plus pull-down) and ~0 in naps and when off. 140 mAh at 1 µA
lasts > 15 years.

## Fine print / gotchas

- **SDB pull-down is mandatory on this board.** The driver is powered from VSYS, which the soft latch does not cut. With
  the MCU off, a floating SDB could enable the chip at 3.6 mA and flatten the cell in ~39 h. R36 holds it off. The
  RP2350's own reset pull-down is not relied on: it is gone when IOVDD is off.
- The SDB rising edge resets the I²C module; registers are kept (p.19). C87 (100 nF) filters glitches that would
  otherwise reset the bus mid-transfer.
- "If VCC has risk drop below 1.75 V but above 0.1 V during SDB low, re-initialize all Function Registers before SDB
  pulled high" (p.19). VSYS collapses only if the PCM trips or the cell is removed. Firmware re-initialises on every boot
  anyway.
- **Command register.** FDh is locked; write FEh = 0xC5 before every page change. The lock re-arms after one write
  (Table 4).
- **Page map.**
  - Page 0: SW1–6 × CS1–30 at (SW−1)·30 + (CS−1).
  - Page 1: SW7–9 × CS1–30 at 0x00–0x59; CS31–39 from 0x5A (Fig. 8).
  - Page 2 (Scaling 1) is laid out like page 0, and page 3 (Scaling 2) like page 1.
  - The figure's CS31–39 cell numbers are partly garbled. We use only CS1–15, which avoids them.
- PWM and Scaling registers default to 0, and GCC defaults to 0. Firmware must set GCC, all 135 SL and SSD = 1, or
  nothing lights.
- SWS = 0000 is the default (1/9), which is what we want.
- Software shutdown does not need the SDB pin; either works for naps.
- **De-ghost:** Table 13 (PG4 02h) sets the SW pull-down (D2:D0, labelled "PUR") and the CS pull-up (D6:D4, labelled
  "PDR"); the labels are swapped relative to the text. The default is 101 = 8 kΩ. "Typically, selecting the 32kΩ will be
  sufficient" (p.18), so firmware writes 0x77. The pulls act only in tNOL1, so they cost no power.
- Open/short detection: set GCC = 0x01 before setting OSDE (Table 14 note).
- PWM frequency: 29 kHz by default. PFS changes it but not the duty (p.17).
- "The IC and LED should be placed far away from any local antenna" (Note 1, p.2): no radio on this board.
- Reflow: 260 °C peak, ≤ 30 s within 5 °C (p.20). The double-sided JLC order reflows it twice.
- Stock is 463 at JLC: enough for 10 boards (and 40), thin for volume.

## Layout guidance (p.19)

- PVCC and AVCC caps close to pins 14, 15 and 25, ground side well connected to chip GND (pins 12, 36, 55, EP).
- RISET close to pin 13, ground side to chip GND (pin 12 is the analog GND, beside it).
- **Thermal pad:**
  - "usually this pad should have 16 or 25 via thru the PCB to other side's ground area".
  - The footprint's paste lanes take 16 vias at (±0.9 or 0, ±0.9) and (±0.9, ±1.8) / (±1.8, ±0.9), 0.2/0.4 mm.
  - Thermally, the realistic 75 mW needs almost none, and the 0.31 W hardware maximum is fine with ~5 (centre lanes)
    plus the B.Cu GND pour and In1.
  - The vias are **via-in-pad**, an open item on gate 7. They are tented or out of paste by construction, but they land
    on F.Cu under the driver's shadow on the front. So their count is a placement decision for the user: 16 per the
    datasheet where the front allows, minimum 5.
- "SWx should have wider trace than CSy" (p.19 item 4).
  - SW nets carry up to 121 mA peak at the hardware maximum (15 dies × 8.1 mA), ~20 mA realistic, for 1/9 of the time.
    That is 40 mA RMS at the maximum, so 0.15 mm is ample.
  - CS nets carry 8.1 mA peak: the board minimum is fine.
- Matrix pins face bottom (SW1–9, CS1–5) and right (CS6–15). Orient that corner towards the ring.
