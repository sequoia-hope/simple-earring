## Current state

Converging, DRC-clean (0 errors / 0 unconnected; silk + dangling-via
warnings only) build of a Ø30.5mm teardrop earring:

- **6 layers** (JLC06161H-3313): F = parts+signal, In1 = solid GND
  (cuts under L1/LX), In2 = VLED annulus + 3V3 disc, **In3 = interior
  signal + a 3V3 pour under the back-east IMU cluster** (bridged to the
  In2 disc by a via at (6.6,-5.6)), **In4 = solid VLED** (feeds the
  interior LEDs anywhere; cut under L1), B = parts+signal.
- **45 LEDs**: 34 perimeter (2.40mm pitch) + 3-LED tip chevron + **8
  interior LEDs in a mirror-symmetric octagon at r 9.2-9.7 around the
  CPU** (chain order: ring → tip → counterclockwise interior from
  top-left). 4 extra bypass caps (C68-C74) sprinkle the octagon.
- **IMU: LSM6DS3TR-C** (LCSC C967633, LGA-14 2.5×3.0) on the BACK right
  tip straight, exactly mirroring SW2. I2C mode 1 (CS/SDx/SCx→3V3,
  SA0→GND ⇒ addr 0x6A). GPIOs chosen for escape-gap alignment (see
  below): SDA=GPIO6, SCL=GPIO11, INT1=GPIO8. Decaps C23 (up-straight)
  + C24 (inboard); pull-ups R14/R15 in the back-east pocket.
- **Back-side passives** (freeing the front interior): R2, R10, R14,
  R15 (east pocket rows, rot 90), R11/R12/C21 (lower-right shoulder
  single-file). R3, R8 stay FRONT (see landmines). All clear of the
  battery insertion sweep — a REAL part on the back must satisfy y<0 or
  |x|>10.5 (courtyard-wise) or sit outside r~11; flush TP pads are
  exempt.
- **Charger status → GPIOs (Aug 2026)**: the charge LED D1 + series R9
  are GONE. LGS4056H CHRG (pin 7) → **GPIO19** (pin 31, private SW exit
  past the NC pins 31-36, committed vias (-4.6,-5.8) + via-in-pad U2.7
  (6.5,4.75)); DONE (pin 6) → **GPIO28** (pin 42, west C5/C22 window,
  committed vias (-4.6,1.35) + (7.8,6.6)). Open-drain active-low,
  internal pull-ups, no new parts. 121 components / 76 nets.
- Right IOVDD decap column is now **three** caps (C2/C4/C6 at
  y -3.6/-1.2/+1.2, 2.4 pitch) with dedicated 3V3 vias; the 10th decap
  (VREG_AVDD's per-pin 100n) was DELETED from circuit.py — C22 (4u7
  CFILT) already serves pin 46, and a 4th cap walled off every escape.

## Hard-won constraints from THIS revision (do not relearn)

- **Pre-verify every placement/via change against locked copper**:
  `build.py --to place && scripts/route.py --no-freeroute` then
  `kicad-cli pcb drc --severity-error` must be 0 errors. An interior LED
  initially sat on the USB pair's front crossing hop at
  (-4.34,-9.40)→(-5.40,-8.75) → freerouting burned every seed.
- **kicad-cli drc without the .kicad_pro next to the board** falls back
  to default constraints (min via 0.5!) and reports garbage. Copy both
  files when probing.
- **0402 courtyards are 1.86×0.94mm** (not body size); BT1's foot-arm
  courtyards reach |y|=3.39, x 10.5-14.09. **SW1's pads (1.2×1.0 at
  x±3.7) stick OUT past its courtyard bbox (x±3.05)** — courtyard
  checks alone will happily park a part on them (seen live, chrg10).
- **The pin-tip corridor next to the QFN is single-lane** (0.65mm), and
  each decap-column gap fits ONE finisher track. Every escape
  (LED_EN pin3→north passage, SDA pin9→middle gap, INT1 pin12→lower
  gap, SCL pin15→south passage) must own its gap with disjoint corridor
  runs — that's why the IMU GPIOs are 6/11/8.
- **BOOTSEL's R3 must stay on the FRONT**: routed to a back-side R3 it
  squats USB_DP's loop lane under the bottom pin row. Channel-via pairs
  for USB_DM/DP are UNFIXABLE (pin order vs available via spots forces
  a same-layer crossing; the only west via slot belongs to 3V3).
- **Interior-LED beautify arcs sweep the r≈9.06-9.76 annulus** between
  neighbors: no pads may sit in the swept angular range (C70, R3 and R8
  placements are all constrained by this). beautify_led_chain now
  samples the TRUE arc, not the chord.
- **R1 rot=270** so its XOUT pad faces the chip side of the single-lane
  crystal channel.
- **Long cross-board nets need a committed B-transition via** once the
  board is this dense: SWDIO (-6.0,-3.1), SWCLK (5.3,-4.6), the four
  IMU/LED_EN signal vias, R14.2's 3V3 via-in-pad. Freerouting plateaus
  ("no improvement since pass N") rather than finishing them.
- **freerouting v2 occasionally live-locks in trace normalization**
  (millions of PolylineTrace.normalize lines on one net) — route.py now
  retries the 900s timeout once.
- **finish.py is layer-aware now**: endpoint layers (F/B/"any"),
  B-side candidates/A*, same-net via anchors, zone-cluster synthesis
  across all layers (union-find over fragments sharing vias), +0.05
  pad-corner margin (capsule model under-covers rect corners), +0.02
  via margin, 0.15mm seg sampling. NEVER truth-test a layer id:
  **F_Cu == 0 is falsy**.
- 6-layer routing time: freerouting takes 6-12 min per attempt (vs 1.5
  min on 4 layers); a converging build is ~25-40 min wall clock.
