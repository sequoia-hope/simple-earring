### The five open nets

| net | span | why |
|---|---|---|
| `VBUS_SENSE` U1.41→R19.2 | 8.2mm | leaves the MCU's jammed west pin window and has to cross the board |
| `LIGHT` U1.40→Q1.2 | 7.3mm | same window, same problem |
| `VBUS` ×2 | — | the tip trunk (3 segs blocked by routed copper) + the short branch to R19 |
| `3V3` | 1.6mm | stub at the MCU |

The two long ones share a cause: pins 40/41 sit at radius 3.45/3.51mm, and BT1's
Ø8 GND pad makes the back inside r4.0 solid copper (a via wants r > 4.4). Both
nets *originate inside the blocked disc*, so they must escape radially on F.Cu
past the coin before B.Cu is available at all — which is why the trick that
rescues this board's other stranded nets (one committed B-transition via at the
pin) does not apply. Exact endpoints: `out/earring-best.json`.

### Board

- **4 layers** (JLC04161H-7628), Ø30.5mm teardrop, USB-C at the tip.
  F = parts+signal, In1 = solid GND, In2 = VLED annulus + 3V3 disc (annulus
  hole pulled to r9.35 so the interior LED ring still gets a plane drop; disc
  9.15), B = holder side + signal. 4-layer is the *timeline* choice; `layers: 6`
  in `design.yaml` is the one-line fallback and the whole pipeline switches on it.
- **45 LEDs** SK6805-EC10: 34 perimeter (2.44mm pitch) + 3-LED tip chevron +
  8 interior in a mirror-symmetric octagon at r 9.2–9.7 around the CPU.
- **U1 RP2350A** (QFN-60) + **U7 W25Q16JV** external QSPI flash.
- **U6 LSM6DS3TR-C** IMU on the BACK right tip straight, I2C mode 1 (addr 0x6A),
  SDA=GPIO6, SCL=GPIO11, INT1=GPIO8 — those GPIOs are chosen for escape-gap
  alignment, see constraints.
- **Q1 ALS-PT19-315C** ambient light sensor on ADC0 (GPIO26, pin 40), FRONT.
- **Charger status → GPIOs**: no charge LED. LGS4056H CHRG (pin 7) → GPIO19
  (pin 31); DONE (pin 6) → GPIO28 (pin 42). Open-drain active-low, internal
  pull-ups.
- **Back-side passives** free the front interior. A REAL part on the back must
  satisfy y<0 or |x|>10.5 (courtyard-wise) or sit outside r~11, so it clears the
  battery insertion sweep; flush TP pads are exempt.

### The rev-2 power path (the headline change)

Rev 1 hung the LDO input and the LED load switch on `VBATT` — the charger's BAT
node — so the LGS4056H's ~41mA program current was shared with the running
system. That is the root cause of both bench mysteries: the cell never charged,
and the ring stayed dark because VLED never reached the SK6805's 3.7V floor.

```
VBUS  --|>|--  VSYS      D3, B5819WS Schottky, ~0.3V at our ~60mA
VBATT --[Q2]-- VSYS      CJ2101 P-FET, on only when no cable is in
VSYS  -> U3 (LDO IN), U4 (LED load switch IN), C18, C29
VBATT -> U2 pin 5 (charger BAT), BT1, C17, SW2   [cell node ONLY]
```

- **Q2's orientation is the whole trick**: source = VSYS, drain = VBATT. A
  P-FET's body diode conducts drain→source, so this way it points cell→VSYS —
  blocking USB from back-feeding the cell around the charger while still
  carrying load if VSYS sags on unplug. Gate = R22 10k from VBUS against R21 1M
  to GND. **R19/R20 pin the dead VBUS node under 0.65V**, which is what turns Q2
  fully on with no cable; that coupling is load-bearing, don't delete or raise
  them without re-checking Q2.
- **The level shifter is not optional.** SK6805 VIH = 0.7×VLED, and on VSYS that
  is 3.29V typical / 3.5V at a 5.25V port — above any 3.3V pad. Q3 (BSS138W)
  is an open-drain inverter with R23 1k pulling LED_D0 to VLED itself. Only
  pixel 1 needs it. Diode-dropping VSYS into the LED window does **not** work
  (the window is narrower than the corners). A pixel with VIH = 0.5–0.55×VDD
  would delete Q3/R23 — see the 48-bit pixel survey in auto-memory.
- **SW2 stays on VBATT deliberately** — on VSYS the R11 divider would read a
  constant 2.35V whenever a cable is in.
- Also carried here from the commits labelled "rev 3": the **USB-or-switch LDO
  enable** (R17/R18/D2 on PWR_EN) and the **VBUS presence divider** R19/R20 into
  ADC1 (pin 41). The rev-2 / rev-3 labels in the git log both describe this one
  unreleased revision.
- **BT1's negative contact is now six mask-defined solder bumps** (Ø1.2 openings
  on r2.8 over the same Ø8 copper, left mask-covered). The first assembled board
  would not hold the cell: 35µm of copper under 20µm of mask stands ~25µm proud
  of a bare pad face, so the coin rode on soldermask. Hand-tinning fixed it on
  the bench; solder only stands proud if mask dams it, and three points define
  the plane the coin sits on, so six on a ring rather than one dome.
- **This retires the R8 22k→15k rework** rather than performing it. 22k already
  gives 40.9mA = the fitted cell's rated 40mA, and with the system off the cell
  the full program current reaches it in *either* switch position.
  `out/rework/` is kept as history.

## Bench truth from the shipped board (rev 1)

- **The board is fully alive**: USB, IMU, LEDs, charger, flash and ADC all pass.
  Gotchas: a post-flash warm reboot needs an SW2 power cycle, and with a battery
  fitted a USB replug is *not* a reset. Flaky USB was a discharged cell — swap in
  a charged one before blaming the board.
- **The fitted cell is an RJD2032C1**, not the LIR2032 the ordered rev named:
  85mAh NMC, 3.6mm thick, flat 40mA charge with no published maximum, IR
  ≤700mΩ. Reconciled Aug 31, and **the cell and the clip are now listed
  separately**: `design.yaml`'s `power.battery` is the one place the cell is
  named (it is hand-fitted and in no netlist), while BT1 — the part JLC
  actually solders — carries the clip's own MPN, `MY-2032-12-R` / C964834.
  The BOM's Comment column used to name a cell against a retainer's LCSC
  number; it agrees with the LCSC column now. **Firmware still lags**:
  `main.cpp`'s power-budget comment sizes the cell at ~40mAh and `chargePct`
  corrects 150mV of IR, both LIR2032 numbers — see open work.
- **"No lights at all" was VLED under the SK6805 floor**, not firmware. Check
  VBATT ≥ 3.7V before suspecting the ring or the code.
- Firmware in `firmware/` (PlatformIO, arduino-pico): ~2× battery life from
  50MHz + a show/nap duty cycle, motion thresholds calibrated against a captured
  wear log (26/122/711 stand/walk/dance, zero overlap). Battery life itself is
  still unmeasured.

## Open work

*Refreshed 2026-10-24 for the LiPo board (afabd86). Gates 0/1/2/4/6 passed; see
`design/gates/`.*

1. **Hot self-start (found by simulation, affects afabd86).** At about 70 °C,
   with the board off, reverse leakage from D2b (BAT54 clamp) and D3 (B5819WS)
   lifts the NCP730's EN past its 0.7 V minimum, and the board turns itself on.
   Fixing either diode alone isn't enough. Candidate fix: D2 → BAV99W
   (drop-in), plus D3 → silicon or an ideal diode. Evidence and the
   temperature table are in `design/sim/button/README.md`. Needs a decision,
   then gates 1/2/6 re-run.
2. **ESD on the edge button (found by the review, affects afabd86).** A
   negative ESD hit on BTN_N acts like a press and pushes ~24 A through D4
   (BAV70W IFSM 2 A). Add a bidirectional TVS beside SW2.
3. **One-button BOOTSEL (proposal, simulated, not adopted).** Delete SW1. A
   10 s hold on the power button forces RUN reset + BOOTSEL, and on battery it
   is a hardware force-off for hung firmware. It passes every scenario and
   corner together with the item-1 fix. It costs 11 small parts, so it needs
   a placement check under the 1.1 mm cell height cap.
4. **Route** the placed board with the new autorouter, then gate 7 (DRC 0,
   0 unconnected).
5. **Gates 8–10**: the rest of simulation/SI (LED rail inrush, charger
   thermal), silkscreen, then release with a sync check and CPL rotations.
6. **Firmware for the LiPo board**: assert PWR_HOLD first thing and enable the
   watchdog; the long-press-off must stay under 2 s if item 3 lands (GPIO21
   then reads active high). Still open from rev 2: `gpio_set_outover(LED_DATA,
   INVERT)` for Q3 (**never flash it to a rev-1 board**), the ALS and
   VBUS_SENSE readers, and battery numbers for the 140 mAh LPR302823.
7. **TPU case for the LiPo stack**: a 4.7 mm stack, so a ≥5.2 mm cup, plus the
   B3U actuator aperture. `gen_case.py` is still dimensioned to rev 1.
8. **Bench checks at bring-up**: a thermocouple on U2 (it sits opposite the
   cell); whether soldering the cell on powers the board up (C27 charges
   through R24, as the sim shows); and CV-phase stability on CHRG/DONE with
   the low-IR cell.

### Roadmap

- **Full-board 3D electromagnetic simulation in Rust, with
  [picoem](https://deepwiki.com/0x4D44/picoem) as the CPU backend.** picoem is
  a cycle-accurate RP2350 emulator in Rust that boots the real bootrom and
  runs real firmware, so the board can be co-simulated:
  - picoem executes the actual firmware image and its GPIO/PIO/QSPI pin edges
    become excitation sources.
  - A Rust 3D field solver (FDTD to start) runs on the stackup and copper
    taken from the KiCad board, and induced pin voltages are fed back into
    picoem's inputs.
  - First targets:
    - QSPI flash SI at the RP2350's clock rates.
    - The LED data line and ring as an unintended antenna (EMC pre-scan).
    - ESD on the edge button.
    - The bootrom's real QSPI_SS sample time, which the button testbench
      currently sweeps from 50 µs to 10 ms because the datasheet gives no
      number.
  - Validate the field solver against openEMS (sources in `~/openEMS-src`) on
    simple structures before trusting it on the board.

## Hard-won constraints (do not relearn)

- **Single hints work, locked paths poison.** Freerouting hangs before pass 1 on
  a fixed *polyline* — any two locked segments meeting at a vertex is enough.
  (The old note blaming "fixed wires that start on a component pad" was wrong:
  CHRG_DONE is a seven-segment fixed chain landing in a pad and it routes.) Give
  it one committed segment or one B-transition via, never a path.
- **Long cross-board nets need a committed B-transition via** once the board is
  this dense: SWDIO, SWCLK, the IMU/LED_EN signal vias, R14.2's 3V3 via-in-pad.
  Freerouting plateaus ("no improvement since pass N") rather than finishing them.
- **Locked pre-route geometry MOVES with the footprints it is derived from.**
  R22's scanned-clean spot failed DRC by 4.3µm because the relaxer settled C16 a
  hair differently and `route.py` anchors the locked CHRG_DONE loop to C16.
  **Re-run the DRC gate after every placement change**, and add scan-found spots
  to `resolve_overlaps`'s EXEMPT set or the relaxer silently invalidates them.
- **Pre-verify every placement/via change against locked copper**:
  `build.py --to place && scripts/route.py --no-freeroute`, then
  `kicad-cli pcb drc --severity-error` must be 0.
- **kicad-cli drc without the `.kicad_pro` next to the board** falls back to
  default constraints (min via 0.5!) and reports garbage. Copy both when probing.
- **`/tmp/freerouting` is shared by every project on this machine** and appends
  across runs. Pass a per-probe `-Djava.io.tmpdir` before reading its log, or
  you will diagnose another board's live-lock.
- **0402 courtyards are 1.86×0.94mm** (not body size). **SW1's pads stick OUT
  past its courtyard bbox** — courtyard checks alone will park a part on them.
  Use `tools/scan_spot.py` (pad-polygon accurate) for real placement, and
  `tools/pad_preview.py` to read landings back: **rotation sense is negated on
  the back side**, never derive it.
- **The pin-tip corridor next to the QFN is single-lane** (0.65mm) and each
  decap-column gap fits ONE finisher track — every escape must own its gap with
  disjoint corridor runs. That is why the IMU GPIOs are 6/11/8.
- **BOOTSEL's R3 must stay on the FRONT**: on the back it squats USB_DP's loop
  lane under the bottom pin row.
- **Interior-LED beautify arcs sweep the r≈9.06–9.76 annulus** between
  neighbours: no pads in the swept angular range (C70, R3, R8 are all
  constrained by this).
- **`finish.py` is layer-aware**: endpoint layers, B-side A*, same-net via
  anchors, zone-cluster synthesis via union-find. **NEVER truth-test a layer id:
  `F_Cu == 0` is falsy.**
- Known-benign warnings: `lib_footprint_mismatch` on R1/C14/C15 (hand-repacked
  crystal pocket) and J1 (shortened pads) — the board copies are truth, a
  footprint re-stamp destroys them.
