Step 3: unload the back (place.py UNLOAD, design.e.yaml unload_back: true)

What was wrong in D. 44 parts overlapped the LED zones (each LED courtyard + 0.3 mm), 133 mm² in all. scan_spot works one side at a time, so it never saw the front LEDs when placing back parts.

What moved. 23 parts were parked, then re-scanned in signal order, each next to the pads it serves. Every LED courtyard + 0.9 mm counted as a keepout, along with U1's back keepout and the silk art. - Test pads: TP1–TP3 are now 1.0 mm round (testpoint_1mm, KiCad TestPoint_Pad_D1.0mm; were 1.5 mm square). They sit beside U1's west and east edges on the back. - IMU cluster: U6 is on the back east of U1, at (8.5, −5.0), off LEDs 29–31. C23 and C24 are at its supply pins. - Flash: U7 + R3 are on the back south of U1, at (1.5, 5.25). Q7 is at (0, 8.75). - Charger: U2 + C17 moved to the front, east of U1, at (6.25, 4.5). That is the only spot off the LEDs near VBUS, PROG and VBATT. It is 0.75 mm tall, in the art-free area. - LDO and soft latch: - U3 is on the back, west. - The soft-latch parts (Q4, Q5, D4, D2, R24, R17) sit west and south-west. - Of those, R24 and R17 are on the front. - Dividers: R11, R12 and C21 sit by U3. R20 is on the front east. C77 is on the front by U8.

Result. 30 parts overlap the LED zones, 57 mm² in all. Of that, 38 mm² is J1 and SW2, which are fixed. The back of the outer ring is clear from LED5 round to LED33. Placed DRC: 0 errors.

Step 1: 0.10 mm rules board-wide (hw/earring/e/earring.kicad_pro)

  • Default and matrix classes: 0.10 mm track, 0.10 mm clearance. Board minimums are 0.10 mm track and 0.10 mm clearance.
  • Other classes: power keeps 0.4 mm track (0.2 mm floor), USB keeps 0.15 mm, and their clearance is 0.10 mm.
  • Vias: unchanged at 0.4 / 0.2 mm, so no JLC extra charge.
  • Fab check: JLC's 4-layer standard is 0.09 / 0.09 mm, and JLC's 0.15 mm SMD pad-to-pad rule stays in the .dru.
  • Currents: the highest current on a 0.10 mm net is 128 mA pulses on the SW lines. That is inside IPC-2221 for 0.5 oz inner copper at ΔT 10 °C (~0.2 A).

  • Step 1 was the big 4-layer lever. With it, all 6 seeds still hit the 900 s deadline while improving.
  • Long runs (2700 s): seed 4 reached 7 open, seed 3 reached 9. Both had 0 DRC errors, with In1 at 572–577 mm², and both still hit the deadline while improving.
  • Seed 4's 7 opens: 4 GND pads that didn't reach the plane, 1 CS, USB_C_DP and VBATT. 4 layers is close but not done; the GND opens might take hand-placed stitching vias.
  • 6600 s runs (2026-11-06), all six seeds: 8, 9, 5, 10, 6, 4 open.
    • Best: seed 6, 4 open (CS14, CS6, USB_C_DP, VSYS) and 2 connection_width necks (SW3, SW7 vias). Seed 3, 5 open (CS10, CS9, RUN_KICK, SW4, USB_C_DP) and 0 DRC errors. Saved as route/earring-e/best/ (seed 6).
    • Seed 4 did worse than its 2700 s run (10 vs 7): the stages are wall-clock bounded, so the same seed lands differently under different machine load.
    • Diminishing returns: seed 6 went 6 → 5 → 4 open at ~3640 s and ~4760 s, then 20+ minutes without a gain; seed 3 dropped one open about every 15–20 min to 5. Both still hit the deadline mid-shove.

    • No GND opens on the two best seeds (they were 4 of seed 4's 7 at 2700 s).
