Design intent — LiPo power source (replaces the coin cell on rev 2)

Phase 0. Drafted 2026-10-22. Signed off by the user 2026-10-22, with R8 = 18k (50 mA) chosen.

This is a delta on rev 2 as described in docs/PLAN.md (commit 4538da2). Everything not named here — MCU, flash, LED ring, IMU, light sensor, USB, the VBUS/VSYS/VBATT power path, 4-layer stackup, outline — is unchanged and keeps its existing intent.

Purpose

Replace the RJD2032C1 coin cell and its MY-2032-12-R retainer (BT1) with a round lithium-polymer pouch cell taped to the back of the board and wired to two solder pads. Goals, in order:

  1. More capacity. 140 mAh against 85 mAh. Correction 2026-10-22: this line first said "in less height, against a 5.3 mm stack". 5.3 is gen_case.py's unmeasured default, sized for the old QIJEY clip model. The rev-2 coin stack is ~3.8 mm (a 3.6 mm RJD2032C1 resting on the board inside a 3.70 mm MY-2032-12-R). The LiPo stack is ~4.4 mm (see Mechanical), so this is a capacity win at a ~0.6 mm height cost, not a height win.
  2. Retire the BT1 copper. The Ø8 centre GND pad and its six mask-defined solder bumps go away, and the back centre becomes ordinary routable area. That pad causes rev 2's 5 unconnected nets (see PLAN.md); removing it should fix them.
  3. Keep the change on main. The coin-cell rev 2 survives only in git history (user decision 2026-10-22).

Open source, personal board. Same as before.

The cell — LiPol Battery LPR302823 (hand-fitted, not in the JLC BOM)

From design/datasheets/LPR302823.pdf (LiPol dwg FD_6110_20, 2020-08-17):

value
outline round Ø28 ±1.0, one flat; flat-to-far-edge 23 ±1.0 (the flat chord sits 9 mm from centre)
thickness 3.0 nominal (part name) / 3.2 (vendor product page). The drawing's side-view dimension reads "28 ±0.5", which is a drawing error. Design for 3.2 and measure the real cell.
leads wires out of the flat, 50 ±3 mm, through the PCM (PCM sits inside the flat end)
capacity 140 mAh min / 145 typ
chemistry 3.8 V "high-voltage" LiPo: operating 2.75–4.35 V, max charge 4.35 V
max charge current 70 mA
max continuous discharge 140 mA
internal impedance < 200 mΩ
PCM overcharge 4.355 V, overdischarge 2.75 V, overcurrent 2–4.5 A
charge temp 0 to +45 °C
mass ~3.0 g
source lipobattery.us, MOQ 5, not on LCSC

Requirements

Power

  • Cell connection: two solder pads on the back, + and −, labelled in copper/silk and placed where the wires leave the cell's flat. No connector.
  • Orientation: the flat faces the USB tip. That is the only orientation where a Ø28 outline clears both J1 and the board edge. With the flat at 9 mm toward the tip, the outline stays ~3.9 mm short of J1's body.
  • Charge voltage stays at 4.2 V (the LGS4056H's fixed CV point). This is safe for a 4.35 V cell and costs some capacity. The datasheet gives no 4.2 V capacity figure; the rough HV-LiPo rule of thumb is ~85–90%, so about 120 mAh usable. Unverified, to be measured on the bench.
  • Charge current: 50 mA, with R_PROG (R8) going from 22k to 18k (user decision). 900/18k = 50 mA (0.36C), which leaves 29% headroom under the cell's 70 mA maximum. See parts/r8-rprog.md.
  • Discharge: the firmware averages ~20 mA (GLOBAL_BRIGHTNESS 8). Any sustained LED pattern must stay ≤ 140 mA total, and that becomes a firmware constraint to document. The PCM's 2 A trip never matters here.
  • Low-voltage floor: the PCM disconnects at 2.75 V. The 3V3 LDO needs VSYS ≳ 3.4 V to regulate, so the firmware still decides when to shut down, and the gauge window moves from 3.0–4.2 V to the LiPo curve. That is firmware work, listed below, with no hardware change.
  • (Superseded 2026-10-24, see the addendum below.) The SK6805 3.7 V floor is unchanged. On battery the ring is only within spec above ~3.7 V, the same as before. USB-powered operation still fixes it. (2026-10-24, user decision: the ring is now WS2816C-1313/4P, 16-bit, with a 3.3 V floor, in three switched zones (left / right / centre). Idle current rises to 0.7–1.0 mA per LED, and only powered zones pay it. Operating temperature is limited to 65 °C. See decisions.md.)
  • The power path (D3/Q2, VBATT/VSYS split, R19/R20 load-bearing for Q2) is unchanged. VBATT is still "the cell node only".
  • Power control: momentary button + CPU-held latch (user decision 2026-10-22, replacing the SW2 slide switch, which the cell covers at every legal angle). A press wakes the CPU, the CPU latches power on, and a long press turns it off, with power dropping when the button is released. Off-state drain is ~0 by construction. Circuit and leakage budget are in decisions.md.
  • Button location: a side-push tact on the tip's straight edge, beside J1 (the SW2 side), actuated from the edge.

Mechanical

  • Cell sits on the back over the round body, attached with double-sided foam tape. Target stack from the back copper to the cell's top (cell 3.2 + 1.5 tape = 4.7, against ~3.8 for the rev-2 coin cell + clip). Revised after gate-4 review: the tape is 3M VHB 4956, 1.5 mm, because foam compresses 30–50% at fitting and 1.2 mm left no real margin over 1.1 mm parts. That puts the target at ≤ 4.8 mm.
  • Parts under the cell outline must be flat. No part taller than the foam-tape thickness may sit under the cell, and nothing with a sharp edge may press into the pouch. The exact height cap and tape product get decided in the phase-4 brief. Today the IMU (0.86 mm), the SOT-323s Q2/D2 (1.1 mm max) and SW2 (1.4 mm) all sit under where the cell will go, so the back placement changes.
  • The power button's actuator is reachable at the tip edge. J1 stays where it is.
  • The TPU case (gen_case.py) needs a new rear cup for the shorter, wider-flat stack. That is a follow-on task, not in this change.

Manufacturing

  • JLCPCB 4-layer, two-sided SMT, as before. BT1 leaves the JLC BOM. The cell is hand-soldered to the pads and taped down after assembly.
  • Hand-assembly step added: solder the two wires, then tape the cell down. The wires are 50 mm long and get trimmed to length.

Firmware follow-ons (not part of this hardware change, tracked here)

  • Power latch (new, required for the board to stay on): drive GPIO20 PWR_HOLD high as the first line of setup() (users hold the button ≥250 ms). Read GPIO21 BTN_SENSE (pull-up, low = pressed, ~0.6 V through the BAV70W). Debounce, and ignore the power-on press until it's released. Long press → drop PWR_HOLD. Enable the watchdog: any reset drops GPIO20, so a hang powers the board off instead of draining the cell. Auto-off at ≥3.0 V (NCP730 is in dropout below ~3 V).

  • chargePct / gauge: LiPo discharge curve, 4.2 V full, IR correction ≤ 200 mΩ × charge current (≈ 10 mV).

  • Shutdown threshold above the LDO dropout and the 2.75 V PCM cut.
  • Power-budget comment at the top of main.cpp: about 120 mAh usable.

Explicit "don't care"

  • Cell swap without soldering. Not required, which is why there's no connector.
  • NTC / thermal charge qualification. The cell has none, and the LGS4056H's TEMP pin stays tied to GND as today.

Known risks / open questions

  1. Cell thickness 3.0 vs 3.2: measure on arrival.
  2. 4.2 V capacity of a 4.35 V cell: measure on the bench.
  3. Placement churn: moving the back-side parts out from under the cell, or capping their height, re-opens placement and routing. That means a full re-place and re-route (the last route took ~2.5 h per attempt).
  4. Wire strain relief: no through-hole is possible (the front is fully populated). The wires rely on tape or kapton over the pads.
