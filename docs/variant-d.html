Variant D: variant C with the driver moved off the LED rings

Parent: variant C (design/variant-c.md): AW20198QNR, 45 × XL-1615, 9 SW × 15 CS, 4 layers. Overlay: design.d.yaml (leds.matrix.spot: tip, arc_triplet). Board: hw/earring/d/. Why: the user's call (2026-11-06). Keep the LEDs, the centred MCU, the button and USB where they are; move the driver anywhere, clear of the LEDs. In C it sat on the back directly under LEDs 8–10 and inner LED39.

Where the driver can go

Each LED is on the front and needs through-vias beside it. So I took every LED courtyard plus a 0.3–0.7 mm margin as a keepout on the back too, then scanned a 5.8 mm square courtyard at every 5° rotation. Two other keepouts applied: U1's back escape keepout (±5.6 × −6…5) and J1.

  • Only one spot fits: the pocket south of J1 and north of U1, at about (−1.75…1.0, −9.5).
  • No room to clear every part off the LEDs. The back's free area off the LEDs is ~203 mm². The movable back parts' courtyards total ~277 mm².
  • Under U1 is out: on variant A's routed board, the keepout is full of escape vias.

Placement (place.py MATRIX_AW_TIP, TIP_MOVED; scan_spot in signal order, LED keepouts on)

  • U11 at (0.00, −9.25), rot 270, back.
  • The SW side faces north toward J1 and the free In2 band. CS1–9 face west, CS10–15 face east.
  • The control pins face south. EN/SCL/SDA sit ~3 mm from U1 pins 27–29.
  • Support parts:
  • C81/C80 (PVCC) go on the front, over pin 5, 1.2/2.4 mm away. J1's courtyard leaves no back spot north of the chip.
  • C83 is on the back at the SE corner. C82 is on the front.
  • R35 (ISET) is on the back at the SW corner.
  • R39, R38 and C87 are on the front, between the driver and U1.
  • C86 (bulk) is on the front, 3.9 mm away.
  • Parts evicted from the pocket:
  • U5 (USB ESD) moved to the front at the tip, on the USB_C_DP/DN front joins (no back spot near J1 was left).
  • R7/R6 moved to the front by U1's USB pins (they're series terminations at the MCU end).
  • C28 went beside the J1 VBUS via. U5's VBUS via, stub and trunk pre-routes are off for this variant (route.py).
  • The BOOTSEL timer moved:
    • Q8 to U1's west edge on the back.
    • R26 to the front.
    • U8 to the front, east of U1.
    • C75 and D5 to the back east.
    • R28 to the front.
  • Placed DRC: 0 errors after route.py's refill. Schematic: 77 nets verified.

  • Moving the driver alone changed nothing. The opens left the driver's pins and spread out. With 0.10 mm matrix rules they are ~12–18 matrix connections (SW and CS about equal) plus ~15–20 other nets. These are mostly MCU signals and power round the board edge: USB, SWD, IMU, BOOTSEL, VSYS/VBATT.
  • The board is capacity-bound on 4 layers with In1 kept whole. The matrix adds 180 LED pins to what variant A routed. The remaining levers:
  • 6 layers. B got 8–10 open on 6, with the driver under the ring.
  • 0.10 / 0.10 mm for every signal class, not just the matrix. JLC's 4-layer standard is 0.09 mm, and round-pendant's p1-66 routed 100 % on 4 layers this way.
  • Unload the back: fewer or smaller back parts under the LEDs (TP1–TP3 are 2.5 mm pads).
  • The 0.1 mm class: CS lines carry 8.5 mA and SW lines 128 mA pulses. 0.1 mm is ample on 1 oz outer and 0.5 oz inner copper (IPC-2221, ΔT 10 °C: ~0.2 A inner).
