Decisions log

2026-10-22 — LiPo replaces the coin cell

  • Cell: LiPol LPR302823 (round Ø28 D-cut, 140 mAh, ~3.0–3.2 mm). User choice from a survey of cells that fit Ø30.5 with ~1 mm margin, PCM included. Alternatives considered:
  • LPR432421: Ø24, 155 mAh, but 4.3 mm thick and no datasheet found.
  • LP401523: 15×23, 100 mAh, 4.0 mm, 100 mA discharge cap.
  • LP402020 / LP502020: 20×21. Easiest to buy, but the 29.0 mm diagonal reaches 30.4 mm at tolerance.
  • Ø30 round family (LPR363027 225 mAh etc.): Ø30 ±1 has no margin on a Ø30.5 board.
  • Adafruit #1317 / #1570 and 20×25 cells: >32 mm diagonal, don't fit.
  • LCSC stocks no LiPo cells (category 57 holds two MEAN WELL packs).
  • Connection: two solder pads, no connector. User choice. A JST-SH socket is ~3 mm tall and ~4×6 mm of back-side area, for a swap convenience nobody needs on an earring.
  • Repo: replace on main, not a variant branch. User choice. The coin-cell rev 2 stays in git history at 4538da2.
  • Flat faces the tip. Geometry: flat chord 9 mm from centre, half-width 10.7 mm. The opposite orientation puts the round edge into J1 unless the cell moves 1.5 mm down, and then the flat-end corners reach r ≈ 15 on a 15.25 board.

  • Design to 3.2 mm thickness. The datasheet side-view dimension "28 ±0.5" is a drawing error. The part name implies 3.0 and the vendor page says 3.2, so take the worse one.

  • R8 22k → 18k (50 mA). User choice 2026-10-22 over keeping 41 mA. LGS4056H gives I_CC as typical only (no min/max), so the headroom under the cell's 70 mA limit is what covers it. See parts/r8-rprog.md.

  • Charge voltage stays 4.20 V; the LGS4056H-4.35 variant is rejected. The cell's PCM overcharge trip can be as low as 4.305 V, below a 4.35 V CV point. This gives up the HV cell's top ~10–15% of capacity, which goes unused rather than being fought for.

  • C16/C17 stay 10 µF 10 V 0603 X5R (gate-1 m1 disputed). At 5 V bias they give ~4–6 µF effective, against the LGS4056H's "10 µF effective". This is unchanged from rev 1/rev 2, both charged correctly on the bench. The LiPo's lower IR (<200 mΩ vs ≤700 mΩ) damps the BAT node less, so watch for CV-phase oscillation on the CHRG/DONE pins during bring-up. If it shows, swap C17 to the 25 V 0805 already in parts.yaml.

2026-10-22 — SW2 slide switch → momentary power button + CPU-held latch

  • Why: SW2 (MSK12C02 slide, ~1.5 mm body per its 3D model; the datasheet has no drawing) sits at r 9.4 from centre, inside any Ø28 cell. A sweep against real copper found no cell angle or offset that clears both SW2 and J1 (gate 4 M1). Putting SW2 under the cell would need ~2 mm tape (5.2 mm stack). User decision: momentary side-push button on the tip edge beside J1; press wakes the CPU, CPU latches power, long press turns it off (power drops on release).
  • Circuit (keeps every rev-2 node downstream of SW_ON unchanged, so D2 / R17 / R18 / R11 / R12 / C21 and the VBSENSE semantics carry over):
  • Q4 CJ2101 P-FET: S = VBATT, D = SW_ON, G = LATCH_G. R24 100k LATCH_G → VBATT keeps it off.
  • Power button SW2: BTN_N ↔ GND. D4 (common cathode; BAV70W after the gate-2 review, first drafted as BAT54CW) A2 = LATCH_G, K = BTN_N: a press pulls LATCH_G to ~0.5 V → Q4 on → SW_ON = VBATT → D2 → PWR_EN → LDO on.
  • Q5 BSS138W: D = LATCH_G, S = GND, G = PWR_HOLD (GPIO20, pin 32), R25 100k pulldown. Firmware asserts it early in boot to hold Q4 on.
  • Button sense: D4's A1 = BTN_SENSE (GPIO21, pin 33, internal pull-up). Pressed → ~0.5–0.6 V (silicon VF) = low against VIL 0.8 V. When the board is off, D4-A1 is reverse-biased (BTN_N sits at ~VBATT through D4-A2 + R24), so nothing back-feeds the unpowered GPIO.
  • Off-state: zero by construction (Q4 gate at VBATT, Q5 off). The leakage that could false-trigger Q4 is D4-A1's reverse current through R24. Revised after gate-2 review: the first draft used a BAT54CW and its 2 µA @25 °C spec, but a Schottky's reverse current at 4 V is ~20 µA by 85 °C (→ 2 V on LATCH_G: latch turns on by itself in a hot car). D4 is now a BAV70W silicon pair (IR ≤ 25 nA @20 V/25 °C, ~1 µA at 4 V/85 °C → 0.1 V) against a ~0.2 V budget (CJ2101 |V_GS(th)| min 0.45 V, less tempco and subthreshold). R24 stays 100k (42 µA while running).
  • C27 100n on BTN_N: ESD and debounce for the user-touched edge button (gate-2 review m3).
  • Firmware must enable the watchdog (gate-2 review M2): otherwise a hang with PWR_HOLD high can't be turned off and drains the cell to the PCM cut. Any reset drops GPIO20, so a crash = power off on battery.
  • USB still boots the LDO via R18 regardless of the latch (unchanged). VBSENSE = 0 on a running board still means "USB-powered, latch off".
  • Firmware obligations (new): assert PWR_HOLD first thing in boot (the user has to hold the button until then); ignore the power-on press until it's released; long press → drop PWR_HOLD (power falls on release); auto-off for low battery (above the PCM's 2.75 V and LDO dropout). On a rev-2 coin board GPIO20/21 are unconnected, so this is harmless there.
  • GPIOs: GPIO20/21 = pins 32/33, the unused west-column run (31 = CHRG). The BT1 centre pad's removal opens the back interior for their escape.

2026-10-24 — power-button simulation (ngspice) and the one-button BOOTSEL proposal

  • Testbench: design/sim/button/. The netlist is generated from circuit.py, the models are datasheet-fitted with limits swept as typ/cold/hot corners, and the RP2350 is behavioral. ngspice comes from conda (mamba create -n spice -c conda-forge ngspice; there is no system package and no sudo).
  • Found a defect in the committed design (afabd86): it self-starts when hot. The board turns on at about 70 °C with nobody touching it. The reverse leakage of D2b (BAT54) and D3 (B5819WS) reaches EN. See design/sim/button/README.md. Not fixed yet: the D3 fix trades USB headroom or footprint, so the user chooses.
  • Rejected: a VBUS-gated strap ("hold the button while plugging in"). With a charged cell, the press powers the board up from battery before USB arrives, so the bootrom has already sampled QSPI_SS.
  • Rejected: RUN double-tap (OTP BOOT_FLAGS1.DOUBLE_TAP). It needs a button on RUN, which would reset the board on every UI press.
  • Rejected: firmware-only BOOTSEL as the only path. It costs zero parts, and arduino-pico's 1200-baud touch already covers day-to-day uploads. But firmware that dies early would then need SWD, and the SWD pads are under the cell. It stays as a convenience on top of the hardware path.
  • Proposed (not adopted): a hold timer → RUN pulse + QSPI_SS strap (one_button() in run.py).
  • Why the P-FET inverter (Q8): D4's diode-OR low is about 0.45 V, too close to the LVC2G14's VT− min of 0.6 V.
  • Why R27 = 10k: firmware that leaves GPIO21's pull-up on must not walk the timer.
  • Why 10 s: the Schmitt VT+ spread (1.3–2.2 V), the X7R tolerance and the input leakage together give a ~4× spread. Across corners it fires at 2.1–8.5 s.
  • Why R28 is 560k and not 1.2M: independent review M1. U8's input leakage has to stay small next to the charge current.
  • Waiting on user sign-off. Adopting it reopens gates 2 and 6.
  • (Superseded 2026-10-24: adopted with R27 4.7k, R28 680k, C76/R29 10n/1M, and Q4 → LBSS84WT1G; see the entries below.)

2026-10-24 — adopted: one-button BOOTSEL, silicon D2/D3, button TVS (user decision)

The user asked "is accidental BOOTSEL temporary or does it erase firmware?" and was told it is temporary: the bootrom only exposes the drive/PICOBOOT, and flash changes only if someone writes a UF2. Their decision: one-button, silicon D3, BAV99W, TVS.

  • SW1 deleted; long hold on SW2 = BOOTSEL / force-off. The one_button() proposal went into circuit.py as simulated, with these changes:
  • R28 560k → 680k. C75 is now the BOM's existing CL10A106KP8NNNC (X5R, basic part). Its corner stacks tolerance, X5R temperature and DC bias (0.70 hot, 1.10 cold). With 560k, the hot minimum fell under 2 s once the bias loss was counted. Window: 2.2–10.3 s, 5 s typical (2.0–19.4 s at U8's II limit, review F3). The user accepted the early end, knowing an accidental trigger only costs a reboot (on battery: off on release) or, with USB in, a dark board until unplug.
  • C77 added (100n U8 decap).
  • D2 → BAV99W (Nexperia, C8662). Same SOT-323 series-pair pinout as BAT54SW, so the footprint and netlist don't change. The EN clamp loosens to VBATT + 0.6 V. That is still legal, because EN is only ever driven to VBATT − 0.6 or VBUS/2.
  • D3 → 1N4007WS (MDD, C193404, SOD-323: the same land, pad 1 = cathode). Rejected alternatives:
  • A higher-barrier Schottky: no datasheet in hand showing the leakage we'd need.
  • A P-FET ideal diode: more parts and no room.
  • R17 → 22k: simulated, not a fix (EN 1.11 V at 85 °C).

The cost is VSYS ≈ VBUS − 0.8 V. That is accepted on the grounds that the ring's 3.7 V floor is only violated on a ≤ 4.5 V port, and only when the LEDs are on. - Button ESD: D6 PESD5V0S1BL at the pad, plus R30 1k. The user approved the TVS. R30 was added because a TVS alone does not help the negative strike: D4 + Q5's body diode (about 1.4 V) conduct before a 5.5 V-VBR TVS does. R30 makes the TVS the only low-impedance path. Simulated: ≤ 22 mA reaches BTN_N, and the strike no longer counts as a press. - D4 BAV70W: Diodes (C93029, 100 left) → Nexperia BAV70W,115 (C28064, 17k). The pinout is the same (Table 2). - 74LVC2G14: Nexperia GW (C6085, 2.8k stock) rather than TI. Same thresholds, but II is ±1 µA where TI's is ±5 µA, and that matters for a µA-level timer. - Placement: see scripts/place.py ONEBTN. Q6/R29 went to the FRONT, into SW1's old spot. After Q7 took the spot under R3, no back spot near RUN would hold a SOT-323. A centre keepout around U1 keeps the back under the MCU free for escape vias. - Firmware has to change with this board: GPIO21 is active high with no pull-up, and the long-press-off must be under 2 s.

2026-10-24 — adoption review fixes; Q4 → LBSS84WT1G (user decision)

  • F2: the latch's hot off-state margin depended on an unspecified CJ2101 tempco. With R24 100k and the CJ2101's 0.45 V minimum threshold, EN reached 0.67 V at 85 °C if the threshold falls 3 mV/°C. Options offered:
  • Q4 → a high-threshold P-FET;
  • R24 → 22k (+140 µA while on);
  • both;
  • accept as is.

The user chose Q4 → high-Vth P-FET: LRC LBSS84WT1G, C383208, 0.8–2.0 V. Q4 passes only ~30 µA, so its higher RDS(on) costs nothing. Q2 and Q8 stay CJ2101 (they need low RDS(on) or don't matter when off). - F4: R27 10k → 4.7k. - F5: C76/R29 100n/100k → 10n/1M. - F1: Q6/R29 moved off the silk art. - F7: D6 moved to C27's spot and C27 moved behind R30. The locked D6-first pre-route is left to the user's autorouter as a constraint. - F6 (silicon D3 on weak ports, DONE may not assert): accepted. It is on the bring-up list.

2026-10-24 — LED ring → WS2816C-1313/4P in three zones (user decision)

  • Part: Worldsemi WS2816C-1313/4P (C42417612, 67k stock).
  • Why: 16-bit per channel plus 4-bit gamma, so the earring's low brightness has fine steps; the user's motivation, with the bigger 140 mAh cell.
  • VDD floor is 3.3 V (SK6805: 3.7 V).
  • Costs:
    • idle current 0.7 typ / 1.0 max mA per LED (SK6805: 0.3);
    • operating temperature only −40…+65 °C;
    • MSL 5a;
    • 0.65 mm tall;
    • 2× frame time and a 280 µs reset (firmware).
  • VIH is 0.7·VDD. I first quoted 0.55 from the A-variant survey, which was wrong, so the level shifters stay.
  • Footprint: the EasyEDA land (0.50 pads at ±0.40), checked against the datasheet drawing. The pin order is the SK6805's rotated 90°, so the diamond ring and the k-loop carry over.
  • Fit check: 1.6 mm courtyards clear each other at the tightest LED spacing (2.33 mm). Only LED44 forced moves: D3 0.25 mm, C29 0.5 mm. The relaxer moved C18 0.15 mm and C26 0.36 mm.
  • Three zones (user chose 3 of 1/2/3/5):
  • left LED1–18;
  • right LED19–37, chained in REVERSE so data enters at the chevron (LED37), beside the left zone's entry;
  • centre LED38–45.
  • Each zone is its own chain + switch + shifter:
  • U4/U9/U10 TPS22913, with R10/R33/R34 pull-downs and C20/C78/C79.
  • Q3/Q9/Q10 + R23/R31/R32. Each pull-up goes to its own zone rail, so a dark zone's first DI is never driven above an unpowered VDD.
  • Rejected: per-zone power on one shared chain. A dark LED breaks the chain, and the still-driven DI back-feeds it through its clamp.
  • GPIOs:
  • Data: GPIO16/17/18 (pins 27–29, adjacent, so one PIO program can clock all three chains in parallel).
  • Enables: GPIO1 (L, existing), GPIO0 (R, pin 2), GPIO22 (C, pin 34).
  • In2:
  • VLED_C is an inner band (r 9.35–10.75) for the octagon.
  • VLED_L / VLED_R split the outer band (r > 11.05) along the line between the zone boundaries.
  • The 3V3 disc is unchanged.
  • route.py: the SK6805-specific freerouting workarounds (C30 via-in-pad, LED1-3/LED32-34 VLED vias, C32→C30 link, LED_D3 fragment) are removed. The board is routed by the user's own GPU autorouter. build.py route (freerouting) is no longer the plan.
  • Locking (user request):
  • The LEDs, J1 and SW2.
  • Also BT1 (cell envelope).
  • Also every part that locked copper or a fixed pour lands on: U1, U2, U5, R14, and the reference VREG cluster L1/C11/C12/C13/R13/C22.
  • Everything else may be nudged by the router.
  • Firmware obligations:
  • Three chains, 48-bit GRB, MSB first, reset ≥ 280 µs.
  • Invert GPIO16/17/18 at the pad (each zone has a Q-inverter).
  • Enable a zone's switch and wait for its rail and the LED's POR (≥ 50 ms precedent, charge-indicator memory) before sending data.
  • A dark zone's data line is harmless either way: its pull-up is dead.

2026-10-25 — 19 parts moved from the front to the back (room around U1)

User request: "free up some space around the CPU and put more components on the back while staying under the foam height."

  • Height limit: the under-cell cap stays at 1.1 mm (brief §11; VHB 4956 1.5 mm tape). Everything moved is at or under it, using datasheet maximums:
  • U2 LGS4056HDA DFN8 0.8 ("0.8 MAX");
  • U3 NCP730 WDFN6 0.80 (A 0.70–0.80);
  • U7 W25Q16JVUXIQ 0.6 (Winbond §10.1 "USON 2x3x0.6"; datasheet now in design/datasheets/W25Q16JV.pdf);
  • U4 TPS22913 0.625;
  • 0603 CL10A106KP8NNNC 0.9;
  • D3 SOD-323 1.10, at the cap. gen_lipo_fit_svg.py treated an unknown height as 0 mm. It now refuses to run when a part under the cell has no HEIGHT entry.
  • Moved:
  • flash U7 + C25 + R3;
  • charger U2 + C16 + C17 + R8;
  • LDO U3 + C18 + C19;
  • D3, C29, R21, R22, R19, R20;
  • LED_L switch U4 + C20, plus R10 (already on the back, moved from the tip to U4). Spots and reasons are in place.py BACK.
  • Kept on the front:
  • decaps C1–C9 and C13;
  • the reference VREG cluster (locked);
  • crystal Y1 + C14/C15/R1, to keep the oscillator loop on one layer;
  • Q1/R16/C26, since the light sensor must face out;
  • the inner-ring caps;
  • Q3/R23. The back top-left has no SC-70 spot anywhere near the LED_DATA_L → LED1 line (best legal spot was 21 mm of total reach).
  • Placement choices:
  • U7 sits just south of the QSPI pin row. At rot 180 its near row carries SCLK/SD0/SD3 (a via and ~3 mm each). SD1/SD2//CS are on the far row and wrap round the body (~6 mm). That holds for either row-facing rotation, and keeping SCLK on the near side is the better half. (Corrected after review: the first draft said every QSPI net was under 4 mm.)
  • R3 sits at U7's /CS pin. QSPI_SS is the fast side and keeps a short stub; BOOTSEL_SW (slow, behind 1k) carries the distance to Q7.
  • U4 moved west, by the VLED_L outer-band half it feeds. Its LED_EN_L is a slow GPIO that crosses from pin 3. The alternative, east beside U9, would have run the zone's supply ~12 mm west instead.
  • U3's EN faces R17/D2.
  • The charger, D3, R22 and the R19/R20 tap sit on one short VBUS run by C16.
  • R19/R20 keep their pads out of U1's back keepout. The first spot put R20 under U1's SE corner, where pins 1–3 escape (review). C18 sits on U3's VSYS pin, between U3.6 and U4.A2 (review: it had been 2.5 mm away with EN/NC between).
  • The back keepout under U1 (about ±5.6 × −6…5) is kept clear for the chip's escape vias.
  • route.py: these freerouting assists were removed because they were measured off the old front spots:
  • CHRG / CHRG_DONE escape vias and U2's locked landing loop;
  • U3's scanned 3V3 via (and the now-unused via-spot helpers);
  • the five QSPI straggler vias;
  • the LED_EN_L channel via. U2 is no longer locked, because nothing locked lands on it any more.
  • Thermal note: the charger now sits under the cell. Dissipation is (VBUS − VBAT) × 50 mA ≈ 65 mW typical and 110 mW worst (5.25 V port, 3.0 V cell). Plus D3: on USB, all VSYS current (MCU plus every lit LED) goes through it, so about 0.85 V × 0.14 A ≈ 0.12 W at the brief's ceiling, 4.5 mm from U2. The charger's own thermal regulation protects the charger, not the pouch. Firmware should cap brightness while charging, and D3 / U2's EP get thermal vias to In1 GND. On a 30 mm board, the rise is set by the whole board's ~70 K/W to air, not by which side the part is on. Bring-up check: cell-surface temperature while charging, with the LEDs lit (the LiPo's charge limit is 45 °C).
  • Result:
  • front parts within r < 10: 40 → 26;
  • front courtyard area within r < 10: 107 → 57 mm²;
  • DRC 0 errors, 274 unconnected (unrouted);
  • no new silk-to-silk overlaps;
  • cell fit: no clash.

2026-11-02 — route.py seeds KiCad's UUIDs

route.py's output changed from run to run. Nothing was random in our script: each new pour and track gets a random KiCad UUID, and KiCad saves the board sorted by UUID, so the order of pours and tracks in the file changed on every run. The router therefore saw a different input each time, and "seed 0" did not make the route repeatable. route.py now calls pcbnew.KIID.SeedGenerator(0) right after it loads and wipes the board. It has to come after the load, because loading also draws from the generator. Three --no-autoroute runs, starting from both the routed and the pre-routed board, now give byte-identical files. Found while ultraroute bisected request #4.
