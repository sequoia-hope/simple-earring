Variant B: matrix-driven RGB LEDs (study, 2026-11-03)

User request: an alternate earring that drives plain RGB LEDs from a matrix driver, as round-pendant does, in place of 45 WS2816C pixels. Same LED count for now, room to add more later. Drivers on the back, the MCU and small parts on the front. This note covers the architecture, the fit, the cost and the battery life. No board yet.

Architecture

The design follows round-pendant (~/pcb/round-pendant/design/architecture.md, parts/drivers.md, parts/leds.md).

  • Driver: one Lumissil IS31FL3741A (C2940604), a 9 SW × 39 CS matrix, I²C to 1 MHz, QFN-60 7 × 7 × 0.80 mm max. It is powered from VSYS, as the ring is today.
  • With 45 LEDs it scans only SW1–SW5 (SWS = 0100, 1/5 duty, ds p.14), using 27 of 39 CS.
  • Growth with no hardware change: 9 SW gives 81 RGB; all 39 CS gives 117.
  • LEDs: XINGLIGHT XL-1010RGB-YG (C965834), 1 × 1 mm, common anode. They are round-pendant's audited part, and smaller than the WS2816C's 1.3 mm.
  • Dimming: 8-bit PWM per LED × 8-bit scaling per LED × 8-bit global current (GCC).
  • Setting GCC for the earring's dim range puts all 256 PWM steps across the levels actually used, so the fine low-brightness steps the WS2816C was chosen for (decisions.md 2026-10-24) carry over.
  • Per-LED scaling handles colour balance without spending PWM resolution.
  • PWM runs at 29 kHz, so no visible flicker.
  • What goes:
  • the three zone switches U4/U9/U10 (TPS22913, WCSP), with R10/R33/R34 and C20/C78/C79;
  • the three level shifters Q3/Q9/Q10, with R23/R31/R32;
  • the 23 LED bypass caps;
  • the VLED_L/R/C nets and their In2 pours.

The zones existed only because each idle WS2816C draws ~0.7 mA. With a driver, a dark LED draws nothing and the whole matrix shuts down to 0.5 µA through SDB. - What comes: - Driver support: PVCC 2 × (1 µF + 100 nF) + 22 µF bulk; AVCC 1 µF + 100 nF; RISET. - SDB: 100 kΩ + 100 nF. - INTB: a 100 kΩ pull-up. - I²C: a pull-up pair on its own bus (I²C0, 1 MHz with 1 kΩ).

Net: about 38 parts out and 10 in. - GPIO: this frees LED_DATA_L/R/C and LED_EN_L/R/C (six pins) and uses SDA, SCL, SDB and INTB, so two pins come free.

Fit

Checked against the current placement with the 38 parts removed (scratchpad/variantb/fit.kicad_pcb, scan_spot.py --art 0.15 --cy-margin 0.1).

  • The driver does not drop in. There is no legal 7 × 7 spot on either side as the other parts stand.
  • The only free square big enough for its 8.2 mm courtyard is 8.75 mm, directly behind U1. U1's nine exposed-pad thermal vias, and the VDD_CORE square vias, come through to the back there. Those vias would land in the driver's GND pad, and the VDD_CORE ones would short.
  • A back re-pack looks feasible on area.
  • Back courtyards cover 275 mm² of ~767 mm² usable, before removals.
  • The driver plus its passives add ~75 mm².
  • That means re-placing the back's small parts (power path, latch, sense dividers) around a driver spot, likely at the south or north of the cell area.
  • The front loses 23 bypass caps and gets smaller LEDs, so it decongests.
  • Height. The QFN is 0.80 mm max, under the LiPo's 1.1 mm cap.
  • Routing is the real risk.
  • Every LED has its anode on a SW line and three cathodes on CS lines.
  • Planned mapping: 9 SW arcs of 5 adjacent LEDs, so the SW nets stay local, and 15 CS lines running as a bus round the ring. That is 1/9 duty, still well inside the LED and driver limits at the earring's currents.
    • The bus is 15 lines at 0.254 mm pitch, ~3.8 mm wide.
    • Split over In2 and B.Cu, it is ~1.9 mm per layer.
    • In2 is free, because the VLED pours go.
  • Alternative: 5 SW × 27 CS, which halves the peak current but makes a 27-line bus.

Battery life (140 mAh LPR302823, ~130 mAh usable; estimates, not measured)

Assumptions: - MCU + flash + LDO awake at 50 MHz: ~10 mA. - IMU: ~0.5 mA. - LED drive at the firmware's dim levels: ~2 mA average, which is the same either way. - WS2816C idle: 0.7 mA typ (1.0 max) per powered LED, so 31.5 mA for 45. - IS31FL3741A: 3.6 mA typ while on, 0.5 µA in shutdown. - Show duty: from the Aug 25 wear log, the share of time the LEDs are on is 25 % standing, 49 % walking and 82 % dancing. Today's naps exist to cut LED idle current.

  • Variant B is 1.6× to 2.7× longer at the same duty. With the IS31FL3733 (2 mA), the always-on case is 14.5 mA → 9.0 h.
  • Off is unchanged. The soft latch takes everything to zero load either way.
  • The next lever is the MCU, at ~10 mA, now the largest load. Both LED types hold their last frame, so the MCU could sleep between frames on either board. With the matrix, always-on could reach roughly 15 h. Firmware, unmeasured.
  • Brightness per mA is similar but not equal. The WS2816C gives 90/190/25 mcd at its fixed ~3.5 mA per channel. The XL-1010's green is roughly 22–40 mcd/mA from its p.6 curve. LED drive is the small term here, so this moves the totals by well under 1 mA.

Phase 1 audit results (variant B)

Audited 2026-11-03: design/parts/is31fl3741a.md and design/parts/xl-1010rgb.md. The datasheets are in design/datasheets/, listed in sources.csv. Both parts pass sourcing: extended, normal lifecycle, JLC stock 463 and 12,478. The independent review is still to do.

Matrix: keep 9 SW × 15 CS at 1/9 duty. - Supply peak is the same either way. It is 135 × 1.125 × ILED (~121 mA at the hardware maximum) for any shape: 5 × 27 halves only the per-die peak, which is already far under the LED's 20 mA continuous rating. - 9 × 15 routes better: a 15-line bus instead of 27, all matrix pins on two sides of the QFN (SW1–9, CS1–15), and room to grow to 117 LEDs. - Ghosting is a wash between the two shapes. - What 5 × 27 would buy: only ~0.1 V of headroom, and only at full scale.

RISET = 47 kΩ 1 % (C25792, basic). - Current: IOUT 8.09 mA, average 0.80 mA per die at full scale. - Hard cap: full white is 108 mA (116 at +8 %), or 123–131 mA with the rest of the system, so even a firmware bug stays inside the cell's 140 mA rating. - PWM use: the brightest level the firmware needs (charge-indicator red, lowest bin) lands at PWM ≈ 227 with GCC = 255. The drip show runs at GCC ≈ 100. - Thermal: the driver dissipates ~75 mW at the firmware's realistic maximum and 0.31 W at the hardware maximum on USB, about +31 °C worst case.

Variant B is still 1.5–2.3× the current board at the same duty, and 2.2–2.4× with no naps against 3.0 h. - Off is not zero load. The driver sits on VSYS, which the soft latch does not cut. With SDB held low it draws 0.5 µA typ and 1 µA max, more than 15 years from 140 mAh. Negligible, but it makes the SDB pull-down mandatory: a floating SDB would cost 3.6 mA with the earring off. - Naps: SSD = 0 or SDB low gives 0.5 µA with registers kept, so naps cost the driver nothing. - Cost: about 12 passives come in (13 with an INTB resistor), not ~10. That is still ≈ +$0.03, so the net stays at ≈ +$0.1 per board. - Reverse voltage: off LEDs see up to VSYS during the 2 µs de-ghost blanking, 4.95 V on USB against the LED's 5 V VR. That is inside the rating but thin, and current-limited to ≤ 77 µA.

Open questions: - IOUT accuracy at 47 kΩ: only 10 kΩ is characterised. - Red efficiency below 10 mA: the datasheet curve is unusable there. - The driver's SDA VOL is unspecified. - How many EP vias to fit. - The reel Vf/Iv bins.

Routing attempts (2026-11-03)

Ultraroute 99bb7ca, with variant A's recipe args. A seed sweep on B's own input, each route judged by KiCad after a refill.

  • CS bus: 15 CS lines round the ring, 5-LED SW arcs, timeout 480 s.
  • Every seed (1–6) left 78–84 connections open, about 47 of them CS and 11 SW.
  • Fanout: 79 of 85 escaped. Negotiation: 231 of 320 connections.
  • SW bus: 9 SW lines round the ring, 9-LED CS arcs (bus: sw), timeout 900 s.
  • Every seed left 48–55 open, about 13 CS and 19 SW, plus scattered other nets.
  • The map change helped, but it's nowhere near routable.

Conclusion: placement fits, but a 4-layer board with the driver on the back-west doesn't route with the free router. The matrix needs either structure (a pre-routed ring bus) or more layers. - Locked In2 ring bus (9 concentric SW rings, bus_rings), 4 layers, timeout 900 s: - Every seed left 71–82 open, which is worse. - The 3.4 mm band blocks every other net's vias, and fanout failures went from 6 to 15. - Switched off. - 6 layers (board.layers: 6, no ring bus), timeout 900 s: - Every seed left 8–10 open: CS 2–5, SW 1–4, the rest scattered. - Negotiation got 293 of 315 connections. Fanout: 81 of 85 escaped. - That's the range variant A was in before its seeds and pre-routes were tuned. - Conclusion: variant B is a 6-layer board, unless every LED stub and via is laid out by hand on 4 layers.
