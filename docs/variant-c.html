Variant C: AW20198QNR + XL-1615 matrix (2026-11-05)

User request: "for our discrete driver work, see the lower density trapezoidal boards under round-pendant and adopt that driver chip and the slightly larger led". Those boards are round-pendant's skewed-parallelogram family (~/pcb/round-pendant/design/study/parallelogram/README.md). Its low-density tiers (p1-66, p2-132) route on 4 layers with an Awinic AW20198QNR. The study names the XL-1615RGBC-RF as the larger, cheaper LED for those pitches.

Variant C is variant B with those two parts swapped in. B (IS31FL3741A + XL-1010) is left as it was. Everything is selected by design.c.yaml (python3 build.py --variant c), through leds.matrix.driver and leds.part.

Cost (per board, at 10 boards)

  • Driver: $3.31 → ~$0.89, so −$2.4.
  • LEDs: 45 × ($0.031 → ~$0.015), so −$0.7.
  • Four fewer passives: about −$0.01.
  • Net: about −$3.1 against B, so about −$3.0 against variant A (B was ≈ +$0.1 over A).
  • Both new parts are extended, as their predecessors were. JLC stock: 6,055 drivers, 718,328 LEDs.

Battery

Against B's corrected table (variant-b-matrix.md), the driver draws 1.8 mA less whenever it is active: - about 0.5 mA less standing, 0.9 walking, 1.5 dancing (same naps); - 1.85 mA less with no naps, which takes no-naps from 6.5–7.3 h to roughly 7.2–8.1 h.

The XL-1615's red is weak below ~10 mA. Red/purple shows therefore sit at the top of B's LED-current range, and firmware should dim with PWM, not SL/GCC. Off stays negligible: 1.3 µA.

Placement (2026-11-05)

  • Driver spot. On the back, a 5 × 5 driver fits clear of U1's escape area in one pocket only: the west, x −12.25…−10.75 (scan_spot.py --art 0.15 --cy-margin 0.1, U1 keepout).
  • U11 sits at (−10.75, 0.75), rot 0 back.
  • Its SW side faces the ring (west), and its control side (VCC, EN, I²C, ISET) faces the MCU.
  • Support parts. Scanned in signal order, 1–3 mm from their pins (place.py MATRIX_AW). The exception is the 10 µF bulk cap, ~4.5 mm south.
  • Moved parts. B's moved parts (MATRIX_MOVED) keep their B spots.
  • Placed-board DRC: 0 errors. The warnings are the same as B's (silk crop, J1 and LED library-mismatch flags).
  • Not done yet: gate 6 review, routing (gate 7), and a 3D model for the XL-1615 (pcbview shows bare pads).

Routing on 4 layers (2026-11-05)

Ultraroute 99bb7ca on the committed placed board (route.py's pre-routes are already on it: 321 open going in). Each result was judged by KiCad DRC with the project rules after route.py's 0.2 mm refill. The scratch boards aren't kept; the args are below.

  • With the GND plane kept (--planes In1.Cu), C doesn't route. It does beat B on 4 layers (48–55 open), but only narrowly.
  • The opens cluster at U11's west SW pins (x ≈ −13.1), which sit under the ring LEDs, and on the 9 SW lines that run the full ring.
  • The SW lines crowd out MCU nets: USB_DM, SWDIO, CHRG and the driver's I²C.
  • Letting the router use In1 gets close (5 open), but only by spending the GND plane.
  • 43 nets and ~570 mm of track go onto In1, leaving about a third of the plane.
  • That plane is the reference under the USB pair and the RP2350's regulator loop (variant A's whole layer plan rests on it).
  • Raising --pour-cost doesn't keep it: every run that's allowed onto In1 ends near 220 mm² and leaves more open.
  • So it's not a 4-layer board with a plane, and not acceptable as is.
  • Conclusion: as placed, variant C needs either 6 layers (B reached 8–10 open there) or a matrix change that unloads the ring. The candidates:
  • an 8 SW × 18 CS map;
  • CS arcs + SW bus moved inboard;
  • the driver moved off the ring's west edge.
