The five open nets

net span why
VBUS_SENSE U1.41→R19.2 8.2mm leaves the MCU's jammed west pin window and has to cross the board
LIGHT U1.40→Q1.2 7.3mm same window, same problem
VBUS ×2 — the tip trunk (3 segs blocked by routed copper) + the short branch to R19
3V3 1.6mm stub at the MCU

The two long ones share a cause: pins 40/41 sit at radius 3.45/3.51mm, and BT1's Ø8 GND pad makes the back inside r4.0 solid copper (a via wants r > 4.4). Both nets originate inside the blocked disc, so they must escape radially on F.Cu past the coin before B.Cu is available at all — which is why the trick that rescues this board's other stranded nets (one committed B-transition via at the pin) does not apply. Exact endpoints: out/earring-best.json.

Board

  • 4 layers (JLC04161H-7628), Ø30.5mm teardrop, USB-C at the tip. F = parts+signal, In1 = solid GND, In2 = VLED annulus + 3V3 disc (annulus hole pulled to r9.35 so the interior LED ring still gets a plane drop; disc 9.15), B = holder side + signal. 4-layer is the timeline choice; layers: 6 in design.yaml is the one-line fallback and the whole pipeline switches on it.
  • 45 LEDs SK6805-EC10: 34 perimeter (2.44mm pitch) + 3-LED tip chevron + 8 interior in a mirror-symmetric octagon at r 9.2–9.7 around the CPU.
  • U1 RP2350A (QFN-60) + U7 W25Q16JV external QSPI flash.
  • U6 LSM6DS3TR-C IMU on the BACK right tip straight, I2C mode 1 (addr 0x6A), SDA=GPIO6, SCL=GPIO11, INT1=GPIO8 — those GPIOs are chosen for escape-gap alignment, see constraints.
  • Q1 ALS-PT19-315C ambient light sensor on ADC0 (GPIO26, pin 40), FRONT.
  • Charger status → GPIOs: no charge LED. LGS4056H CHRG (pin 7) → GPIO19 (pin 31); DONE (pin 6) → GPIO28 (pin 42). Open-drain active-low, internal pull-ups.
  • Back-side passives free the front interior. A REAL part on the back must satisfy y<0 or |x|>10.5 (courtyard-wise) or sit outside r~11, so it clears the battery insertion sweep; flush TP pads are exempt.

The rev-2 power path (the headline change)

Rev 1 hung the LDO input and the LED load switch on VBATT — the charger's BAT node — so the LGS4056H's ~41mA program current was shared with the running system. That is the root cause of both bench mysteries: the cell never charged, and the ring stayed dark because VLED never reached the SK6805's 3.7V floor.

VBUS  --|>|--  VSYS      D3, B5819WS Schottky, ~0.3V at our ~60mA
VBATT --[Q2]-- VSYS      CJ2101 P-FET, on only when no cable is in
VSYS  -> U3 (LDO IN), U4 (LED load switch IN), C18, C29
VBATT -> U2 pin 5 (charger BAT), BT1, C17, SW2   [cell node ONLY]
  • Q2's orientation is the whole trick: source = VSYS, drain = VBATT. A P-FET's body diode conducts drain→source, so this way it points cell→VSYS — blocking USB from back-feeding the cell around the charger while still carrying load if VSYS sags on unplug. Gate = R22 10k from VBUS against R21 1M to GND. R19/R20 pin the dead VBUS node under 0.65V, which is what turns Q2 fully on with no cable; that coupling is load-bearing, don't delete or raise them without re-checking Q2.
  • The level shifter is not optional. SK6805 VIH = 0.7×VLED, and on VSYS that is 3.29V typical / 3.5V at a 5.25V port — above any 3.3V pad. Q3 (BSS138W) is an open-drain inverter with R23 1k pulling LED_D0 to VLED itself. Only pixel 1 needs it. Diode-dropping VSYS into the LED window does not work (the window is narrower than the corners). A pixel with VIH = 0.5–0.55×VDD would delete Q3/R23 — see the 48-bit pixel survey in auto-memory.
  • SW2 stays on VBATT deliberately — on VSYS the R11 divider would read a constant 2.35V whenever a cable is in.
  • Also carried here from the commits labelled "rev 3": the USB-or-switch LDO enable (R17/R18/D2 on PWR_EN) and the VBUS presence divider R19/R20 into ADC1 (pin 41). The rev-2 / rev-3 labels in the git log both describe this one unreleased revision.
  • BT1's negative contact is now six mask-defined solder bumps (Ø1.2 openings on r2.8 over the same Ø8 copper, left mask-covered). The first assembled board would not hold the cell: 35µm of copper under 20µm of mask stands ~25µm proud of a bare pad face, so the coin rode on soldermask. Hand-tinning fixed it on the bench; solder only stands proud if mask dams it, and three points define the plane the coin sits on, so six on a ring rather than one dome.
  • This retires the R8 22k→15k rework rather than performing it. 22k already gives 40.9mA = the fitted cell's rated 40mA, and with the system off the cell the full program current reaches it in either switch position. out/rework/ is kept as history.

Bench truth from the shipped board (rev 1)

  • The board is fully alive: USB, IMU, LEDs, charger, flash and ADC all pass. Gotchas: a post-flash warm reboot needs an SW2 power cycle, and with a battery fitted a USB replug is not a reset. Flaky USB was a discharged cell — swap in a charged one before blaming the board.
  • The fitted cell is an RJD2032C1, not the LIR2032 the ordered rev named: 85mAh NMC, 3.6mm thick, flat 40mA charge with no published maximum, IR ≤700mΩ. Reconciled Aug 31, and the cell and the clip are now listed separately: design.yaml's power.battery is the one place the cell is named (it is hand-fitted and in no netlist), while BT1 — the part JLC actually solders — carries the clip's own MPN, MY-2032-12-R / C964834. The BOM's Comment column used to name a cell against a retainer's LCSC number; it agrees with the LCSC column now. Firmware still lags: main.cpp's power-budget comment sizes the cell at ~40mAh and chargePct corrects 150mV of IR, both LIR2032 numbers — see open work.
  • "No lights at all" was VLED under the SK6805 floor, not firmware. Check VBATT ≥ 3.7V before suspecting the ring or the code.
  • Firmware in firmware/ (PlatformIO, arduino-pico): ~2× battery life from 50MHz + a show/nap duty cycle, motion thresholds calibrated against a captured wear log (26/122/711 stand/walk/dance, zero overlap). Battery life itself is still unmeasured.

Evidence: design/sim/button/README.md, design/gates/phase{1,2,6,8}.md, design/decisions.md.

  1. LED ring → WS2816C-1313/4P in three zones (branch ws2816-zones, 2026-10-24): left LED1–18, right LED37→19, centre LED38–45. Each zone has its own TPS22913, shifter and In2 plane piece. Data GPIO16/17/18, enables GPIO1/0/22. Placed, DRC 0; routing is the user's GPU autorouter (no freerouting). Locked: the LEDs, J1, SW2, BT1, U1, U5, R14, Q1 and the VREG cluster (U2 unlocked 2026-10-25: nothing locked lands on it now).
  2. 19 parts front → back (branch back-side-move, 2026-10-25) to clear the front around U1: flash U7/C25/R3, charger U2/C16/C17/R8, LDO U3/C18/C19, D3/C29/R21/R22/R19/R20, LED_L switch U4/C20/R10. All ≤ 1.1 mm under the cell. Front courtyard area within r < 10: 107 → 57 mm². DRC 0.
  3. Firmware has to change with the hardware. LEDs: three 48-bit chains (GRB, MSB first, reset ≥ 280 µs), GPIO16/17/18 inverted at the pad, and a zone's enable raised ≥ 50 ms before its first frame. Idle each data GPIO HIGH (inverted, so DI idles low through the 280 µs reset). Power zones off, don't just blank them: 45 dark-but-powered WS2816C draw 31.5–45 mA, which is 3–4.4 h on the 140 mAh cell by itself. Keep a hard brightness cap, since full white is ~0.47 A, 3.4× the cell's 140 mA rating. Cap it lower while on USB: U2 and D3 (which then carries all the LED current) sit under the cell since 2026-10-25. GPIO21 is now active high with no pull-up. The long-press-off must be under 2 s. Assert PWR_HOLD first thing and enable the watchdog.
  4. Bring-up checks for the new parts: a zone that has been switched off re-accepts data without a full power cycle (TPS22913B has no output discharge, so a dark rail floats down through the LEDs' own IDD); U8 input leakage (the timer assumes nA; at the 1 µA spec max the earliest fire drops to ~1.9 s), the real BOOTSEL hold time on a few boards, VSYS on a weak USB port (silicon D3: about VBUS − 0.8 V), and whether DONE asserts on a ≤ 4.6 V port with a full cell. There, Q2's body diode lets the cell share the load, so the charge current may never fall to the C/10 termination level. Charging itself is unaffected: U2 runs straight off VBUS (UVLO 4.0 V, 1.2 Ω pass FET).
  5. Routing constraints:
  6. Route BTN_EXT as SW2.2 → D6.1 → R30.1, with nothing else on the net.
  7. Put a GND via within 1 mm of D6.2.
  8. Keep RUN_KICK (a 1 MΩ node) clear of LED_DATA.
  9. Two courtyards sit 0.03–0.07 mm from the 1.6 mm LED courtyards (LED35–C64, LED42–C26). Nudge C64 and C26 only away from the ring.
  10. Keep the back directly under U1 (about ±5.6 × −6…5) for the chip's escape vias. U7 sits just south: SCLK/SD0/SD3 reach its near row, SD1/SD2//CS its far row (they wrap round the body). SD0/SD3 cross between U1 and U7, so swap them on the via layer.
  11. LED_EN_L crosses under U1 from pin 3 (east) to U4 (west), and VBUS_SENSE runs from R19/R20 (east) to pin 41 (west). Both are slow; keep VBUS_SENSE over In1 GND, clear of the QSPI vias.
  12. D3 and U2's EP want thermal vias into In1 GND (both are under the cell).
  13. R3 sits at U7's /CS: keep the QSPI_SS stub short and let BOOTSEL_SW (behind 1k, slow) run the distance to Q7.
  14. Q9/R31 (right-zone shifter) sit on the back west of the zone cut, about 10 mm from LED37. Fine electrically; move them nearer the chevron if routing wants it.
  15. Route the placed board with the new autorouter, then gate 7 (DRC 0, 0 unconnected).
  16. Gates 8–10: the rest of simulation/SI (LED rail inrush, charger thermal), silkscreen, then release with a sync check and CPL rotations.
  17. Firmware for the LiPo board (see item 1). Still open from rev 2: gpio_set_outover(LED_DATA, INVERT) for Q3 (never flash it to a rev-1 board), the ALS and VBUS_SENSE readers, and battery numbers for the 140 mAh LPR302823.
  18. TPU case for the LiPo stack: a 4.7 mm stack, so a ≥5.2 mm cup, plus the B3U actuator aperture. gen_case.py is still dimensioned to rev 1.
  19. Bench checks at bring-up: a thermocouple on the cell over U2 (U2 is under the cell since 2026-10-25; ~65–110 mW while charging, plus D3); whether soldering the cell on powers the board up (C27 charges through R24, as the sim shows); and CV-phase stability on CHRG/DONE with the low-IR cell.

Roadmap

  • Full-board 3D electromagnetic simulation in Rust, with picoem as the CPU backend. picoem is a cycle-accurate RP2350 emulator in Rust that boots the real bootrom and runs real firmware, so the board can be co-simulated:
  • picoem executes the actual firmware image and its GPIO/PIO/QSPI pin edges become excitation sources.
  • A Rust 3D field solver (FDTD to start) runs on the stackup and copper taken from the KiCad board, and induced pin voltages are fed back into picoem's inputs.
  • First targets:
    • QSPI flash SI at the RP2350's clock rates.
    • The LED data line and ring as an unintended antenna (EMC pre-scan).
    • ESD on the edge button.
    • The bootrom's real QSPI_SS sample time, which the button testbench currently sweeps from 50 µs to 10 ms because the datasheet gives no number.
  • Validate the field solver against openEMS (sources in ~/openEMS-src) on simple structures before trusting it on the board.

Hard-won constraints (do not relearn)

  • Single hints work, locked paths poison. Freerouting hangs before pass 1 on a fixed polyline — any two locked segments meeting at a vertex is enough. (The old note blaming "fixed wires that start on a component pad" was wrong: CHRG_DONE is a seven-segment fixed chain landing in a pad and it routes.) Give it one committed segment or one B-transition via, never a path.
  • Long cross-board nets need a committed B-transition via once the board is this dense: SWDIO, SWCLK, the IMU/LED_EN signal vias, R14.2's 3V3 via-in-pad. Freerouting plateaus ("no improvement since pass N") rather than finishing them.
  • Locked pre-route geometry MOVES with the footprints it is derived from. R22's scanned-clean spot failed DRC by 4.3µm because the relaxer settled C16 a hair differently and route.py anchors the locked CHRG_DONE loop to C16. Re-run the DRC gate after every placement change, and add scan-found spots to resolve_overlaps's EXEMPT set or the relaxer silently invalidates them.
  • Pre-verify every placement/via change against locked copper: build.py --to place && scripts/route.py --no-freeroute, then kicad-cli pcb drc --severity-error must be 0.
  • kicad-cli drc without the .kicad_pro next to the board falls back to default constraints (min via 0.5!) and reports garbage. Copy both when probing.
  • /tmp/freerouting is shared by every project on this machine and appends across runs. Pass a per-probe -Djava.io.tmpdir before reading its log, or you will diagnose another board's live-lock.
  • 0402 courtyards are 1.86×0.94mm (not body size). SW1's pads stick OUT past its courtyard bbox — courtyard checks alone will park a part on them. Use tools/scan_spot.py (pad-polygon accurate) for real placement, and tools/pad_preview.py to read landings back: rotation sense is negated on the back side, never derive it.
  • The pin-tip corridor next to the QFN is single-lane (0.65mm) and each decap-column gap fits ONE finisher track — every escape must own its gap with disjoint corridor runs. That is why the IMU GPIOs are 6/11/8.
  • (Superseded 2026-10-25: "R3 must stay on the front" was a freerouting lane problem. R3 is now on the back beside U7's /CS.)
  • Interior-LED beautify arcs sweep the r≈9.06–9.76 annulus between neighbours: no pads in the swept angular range (C70 is constrained by this; R3 and R8 were too until they moved to the back on 2026-10-25).
  • finish.py is layer-aware: endpoint layers, B-side A, same-net via anchors, zone-cluster synthesis via union-find. NEVER truth-test a layer id: F_Cu == 0 is falsy.*
  • Known-benign warnings: lib_footprint_mismatch on R1/C14/C15 (hand-repacked crystal pocket) and J1 (shortened pads) — the board copies are truth, a footprint re-stamp destroys them.
