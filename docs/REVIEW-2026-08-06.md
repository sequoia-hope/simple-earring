# Schematic review, 6 Aug 2026 — every chip against its datasheet

Every IC on the board was checked pin-by-pin against the primary datasheet
(not a symbol library, not a prior board), with the figures read visually.
Two of them were wrong, and both would have killed the board.

Sources used: RP2350 datasheet RP-008280 + "Hardware design with RP2350" +
the official `RPI-RP2350A-MINIMAL_R4-S1` KiCad project in `docs/`;
LGS4056H rev C V1.0; NCP730 (onsemi); TPS22913 SLVSB49F; USBLC6-2 rev 2;
LSM6DS3TR-C DocID030071 rev 3; W25Q16JV rev H; SK6805-EC10-000 REV.01;
ABM8-272-T3 via the RP2350 guide §4.1.

---

## 1. FATAL — IMU serial pins were rotated by one (fixed)

`circuit.py` had `U6` (LSM6DS3TR-C) as `12 = IMU_SCL, 13 = IMU_SDA,
14 = 3V3(CS)`. ST's Table 2 and Figure 1 say the trio is:

| pin | function |
|---|---|
| 12 | **CS** — I²C/SPI mode select (high = I²C) |
| 13 | **SCL** |
| 14 | **SDA** |

As built that means:

* **CS was driven by the I²C clock.** Every SCL edge re-selects SPI mode, so
  the part could never complete an I²C transaction.
* **SDA was strapped hard to 3V3.** SDA is open-drain; the moment the IMU
  pulled it low to ACK it would short 3V3 to ground through its own output
  FET. Not merely non-functional — a damage path.

Confirmed three ways: ST's datasheet text, ST's Figure 1 bottom-view pad
map, and KiCad's own `Sensor_Motion:LSM6DS3` symbol. The footprint
(`LGA-14_L3.0-W2.5-P0.50-TL`) was verified pad-by-pad against Figure 1 and
is **correct** — the error was only in the netlist.

Now `12 = 3V3, 13 = IMU_SCL, 14 = IMU_SDA`. Everything else about U6 (SDx/SCx
to VDDIO, SDO/SA0 to GND for 0x6A, INT2/NC floating, 100n on VDD and VDDIO)
was already right.

## 2. FATAL — BOOTSEL button shorted BOOTSEL_SW to GND (fixed)

`SW1` (SKRPAME010, 4-terminal tact) was netted `{1,2} = BOOTSEL_SW`,
`{3,4} = GND` on the assumption that pads 1-2 are one contact.

The footprint puts **pads 1 and 3 on one side** of the body (x = −2.10) and
**2 and 4 on the other** (x = +2.10). A 4-terminal tact is SPST with two
terminals per side, and the two on a side are the same node — so the
contacts are `{1,3}` and `{2,4}`. The old netlist therefore put both nets on
both contacts: **BOOTSEL_SW permanently shorted to GND.** QSPI_SS would have
sat at ground through R3, so the chip would enter the USB bootloader every
boot and never run user code, and the flash /CS would be fighting a 1k
pulldown.

The board confirmed it: two tracks ran straight across the switch body,
`(97.90,87.73)→(102.10,87.73)` on BOOTSEL_SW and `(102.10,89.87)→(97.90,89.87)`
on GND.

Fixed by netting **only the diagonal**: pad 1 = BOOTSEL_SW, pad 4 = GND,
pads 2 and 3 unnetted. Diagonal pads land on opposite contacts under *either*
numbering convention, so this is correct without needing the ALPS drawing
(which is not publicly downloadable). If you confirm same-side pairing, add
3 → BOOTSEL_SW and 2 → GND for the redundant joint.

## 3. Core rail had no HF decoupling (fixed, no new parts)

RP2350 ds §6.1.3: *"the two DVDD pins closest to the regulator should be
decoupled with a 100nF capacitor close to the pins. The DVDD pin furthest
from the regulator should be decoupled with a 4.7μF capacitor."*
VDD_CORE carried only C12/C13 (4.7 µF) — no 100 nF at all. The official R4
minimal board carries three 100 nF on +1V1.

There is no room for new 0402s near the DVDD pins (the back under the MCU is
entirely BT1's footprint; the front decap ring is solid — nearest free slots
were 3.3 mm and 5.25 mm away). But **C5 and C6 were already sitting 1.39 mm
from DVDD 39 and 1.63 mm from DVDD 6** — the two core pins nearest the
regulator — merely netted to 3V3.

They are now on VDD_CORE. IOVDD 38 and 1 fall back on C3 (2.10 mm) and C4:
the same "share a capacitor where the escape fan-out leaves no room" trade
the RP2350 hardware design guide §2.2.1 documents making on RPi's own board.
C13 stays as the 4.7 µF at DVDD 23 — which is, correctly, the DVDD pin
furthest from the regulator (7.2 mm vs 3.4 / 6.1 mm).

## 4. Added — 100 nF at the USBLC6-2P6 (C28)

ST's USBLC6-2 ds §3: the rail-to-rail clamp references VBUS, so where the
run from the VBUS pin to +VCC cannot be kept short they ask for a 100 nF
right at the part. Our only VBUS bulk (C16, 10 µF) is over at the charger.
C28 is placed on B.Cu at (103.14, 87.90), 3.2 mm from U5 — the closest free
0402 slot that crosses only one existing net.

## Verified correct, no change needed

* **RP2350A pin map** — all 61 pins diffed programmatically against RPi's own
  symbol in the R4 reference project. Exact match.
* **VREG loop** — L1 3.3 µH with the polarity dot on VOUT (pad 1) per ds
  Fig 23, C11 4u7 CIN at VREG_VIN, C12 4u7 COUT at the inductor, R13 33R +
  C22 4u7 filtering VREG_AVDD, VREG_FB fed from COUT. Matches the R4 board
  component-for-component. Loop lengths: CIN 1.28 mm, COUT 2.26 mm, L1
  3.88 mm from their pins.
* **Crystal** — Y1 pins 1/3 as the terminals with 2/4 grounded, 15 pF C0G
  load caps, 1 k series on XOUT. Identical to the R4 board. Do not retune.
* **QSPI flash** — W25Q16JV pinout `1 /CS, 2 DO(IO1), 3 /WP(IO2), 4 GND,
  5 DI(IO0), 6 CLK, 7 /HOLD(IO3), 8 VCC` matches the R4 board exactly, plus
  C25 100 n at VCC (1.91 mm).
* **BOOTSEL strap** — QSPI_SS + 1 k + button to GND, Pico-style. Correct
  topology (the bug was the switch pinout, not the strap).
* **USB front end** — USBLC6-2P6 `1/6 = I/O1, 3/4 = I/O2, 2 = GND, 5 = VBUS`
  confirmed from the functional diagram; ESD on the connector side of the
  27 R series resistors; CC1/CC2 5.1 k pulldowns; SBU1/2 unconnected.
* **Charger** — LGS4056H `1 TEMP, 2 PROG, 3 GND, 4 VCC, 5 BAT, 6 DONE,
  7 CHRG, 8 CE`. **TEMP → GND is explicitly correct**: the pin table says
  *"if the TEMP pin is grounded or floating, the battery temperature
  detection function is canceled and other charging functions are normal."*
  (The page-2 note saying "leave floating" is a simplification.) R8 = 22 k
  gives I_BAT = 900/R_PROG = 41 mA, matching the datasheet's sub-300 mA
  formula. 10 µF on VCC and BAT as required.
* **LDO** — NCP730 WDFN-6 `1 OUT, 2 NC, 3 GND, 4 EN, 5 NC, 6 IN, EP GND`,
  1 µF in and out (ds: "stable with small 1 µF ceramic capacitors"), EN
  driven hard both ways by the SPDT.
* **Load switch** — TPS22913B ball map `A1 VOUT, A2 VIN, B1 GND, B2 ON`
  confirmed; 2 A rating, active-high enable with a 10 k pulldown so it is off
  at boot, and quick-output-discharge so the ring cannot glow when off.
* **LEDs** — SK6805-EC10 `1 GND, 2 DIN, 3 VDD, 4 DOUT`, confirmed against
  REV.01 §4/§5. Note this genuinely differs from the SK6805-**EC15**
  (1 DIN, 2 VDD, 3 DOUT, 4 GND) — do not cross-reference the sibling part.
* **IMU decoupling** — C23/C24 100 n on VDD and VDDIO as ST requires.

## Observations worth knowing (no change made)

* **USB series resistors are 14–15 mm from the MCU.** RPi's reference carries
  the note *"Make sure R7 and R8 are close to RP2350"* — the stub between the
  resistor and the chip is what matters. On a 30 mm board with the connector
  at the tip this is hard to avoid, and full-speed USB is tolerant, but if
  you ever re-place, move R6/R7 to the MCU end of the run.
* **LED current budget.** 45 × SK6805 at full white is roughly 0.7–0.9 A.
  The TPS22913 (2 A) and the copper are fine; the LIR2032 is not. Firmware
  must cap brightness — this is a software constraint, not a board defect.
* **SK6805 wants 3.7–5.5 V** and the cell runs 3.0–4.2 V, so the ring dims
  near end of discharge. Known and accepted (best of the in-stock 1010s).
* **VBUS is charge-only.** USB enumeration needs a charged cell and SW2 on.
* The 10 µF 0603 parts (C16/C17, 10 V X5R) derate under DC bias; the
  datasheet asks for "10 µF rms". At 41 mA charge current this is not
  stressed, but a 25 V part would hold up better if you ever re-spin.

## Hand-route punch list

DRC after the changes: **20 errors, 6 unconnected**, all confined to two
spots. Before the review it was 0 errors / 0 unconnected, so everything below
is work this review created — deliberately, per "allow it to break".

**A. U6 serial corner** (19 errors, 3 unconnected). The three nets rotate by
one pad along the package edge, so the existing escapes each land one pad
short. The stubs that sat directly on the pads were removed; the rest of each
run is intact.

* `IMU_SCL` — its run now ends 0.5 mm short; drag the endpoint from pad 12 to
  **pad 13**.
* `IMU_SDA` — same, from pad 13 to **pad 14**.
* `3V3` — needs a new path to **pad 12**. The old 3V3 via at (107.345, 89.719)
  is now sitting on pad 14 (IMU_SDA) and must move. Note pads 9/10/11 are NC
  and unconnected, so there is an open corridor to pad 12 from the east.

**B. C5 / C6 core-rail re-net** (1 error, 2 unconnected). Their old 3V3 stubs
into the 3V3 disc pour were removed. Each needs a short VDD_CORE run — C5 to
U1 pad 39 (1.39 mm), C6 to the VDD_CORE track already 0.58 mm away. One 3V3
track passes 0.42 mm from C6 pad 1 and needs nudging.

**C. C28** (0 errors, 1 unconnected). GND already reaches it through the
back pour; VBUS needs 0.66 mm to the In2.Cu VBUS track.

Zones were refilled (delta −0.01 % on `gnd_front`, which is the pour
correctly pulling back off SW1's now-unnetted pads).
