USB bring-up — where to put the meter  ordered rev 43eadc1 (the board in your hand)  ·  back to dashboard

Symptom: plugged into USB, nothing in dmesg at all — the host never saw a D+ pull-up, so the RP2354A boot ROM never reached USB. Power flows VBUS → charger → VBATT → LDO → 3V3 → RP2354A (which makes its own 1.1 V core with the internal switcher). The LDO enable is gated by the back-side slide switch SW2 — the whole board is dead when it's off, USB or not. Work the numbered steps in order; the first missing rail is the culprit.

✅ Resolved (Aug 25): with a charged LIR2032 fitted the board is fully alive — USB CDC, IMU (clean 1 g vector), charger status and the 45-LED chain all pass. Two contributing faults: the no-battery charger motorboat below (still true), and poor cell − contact — the 8 mm center pad sits below the surrounding mask-over-traces, fixed by tinning the pad proud. This page stays as the bench reference; test firmware lives in firmware/.
Before probing anything: slide SW2 (back side, upper area near the IMU) to its other position and replug. Its as-shipped position is undefined and it gates the LDO — this alone would explain a completely dead board. Expected ON is knob toward the battery (pin 1 end); if in doubt, check step ③ in both positions. And use a known-good data cable — a charge-only lead shows these exact symptoms.
No battery — sequencing matters (finding, Aug 24): with SW2 already ON when USB is plugged, PWR_EN/VBATT was seen motorboating ≈1.8–2.2 V. Below ≈2.9 V the charger trickle-limits to ≈4 mA (a tenth of the 41 mA program), so the LDO + MCU waking at ≈2 V overload it and VBATT collapses and retries — a "wave" on a DMM. Cold-start recipe: SW2 OFF → plug USB → wait for VBATT ≈4.2 V → SW2 ON (full 41 mA is then available; boot ROM needs ≈15–25 mA). If it still sags, fit a charged cell or a ≈4 V supply: side clip lands = +, center pad = −. A discharged LIR2032 clamps VBATT low — remove it. Powered-off resistance sanity check: 3V3→GND and VBATT→GND should each read tens of kΩ or more, not single-digit ohms.

5RUN back: TP1, the lone gold square ⑤ 3.3 V held low → R2 pull-up or a short on RUN; chip is in reset.
61.1 V core front: L1 pad ⑥ (bare inductor land, below-left of the MCU) 1.1 V 3V3 good but no 1.1 V → internal switcher: inspect L1 (the part JLC flagged) and C12 solder.
7USB D± back: beep J1's center pads (dashed ring) ↔ R6/R7 ⑦; then R6/R7 far side ↔ onward (27 Ω in series) ≈0 Ω to the near side, ≈27 Ω through only matters if ①–⑥ all pass: open joint on the fine USB pads or the ESD chip U5.

If every rail is good and it still won't enumerate: hold BOOTSEL (front button) while plugging in, and eyeball the 12 MHz crystal's solder (front, above the MCU) — the boot ROM needs the crystal running before USB works. Blank internal flash should drop into the USB bootloader on its own (2e8a:000f RP2350 Boot in lsusb).
