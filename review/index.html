Layout review — pre-order polish pass (Aug 6, 2026 · commit 4de8d2e)

Fix pass applied: most P1–P3 items below have since been fixed or dispositioned — see the fix report with before/after diffs. Two findings were corrected in the process: the item-2 sliver isolates nothing (downgraded), and item-8's "elbow" is U5's pad entry (withdrawn).

Board state: 0 DRC errors, 0 unconnected. Everything below is polish, ranked by how much I think it matters. Method: detour-ratio + corner/switchback scan of all 1250 segments, per-plane raster erosion with geodesic neck localization (each neck attributed to the nearest offending track/via), island stitch audit, return-path void sampling under critical nets — then every finding confirmed or rejected visually on 40 px/mm renders. All coordinates are KiCad absolute mm (board center ≈ (100, 97.4), +y down), so you can type them straight into the KiCad footer / Ctrl-G.

Big-picture: freerouting used In1 and In2 as signal layers. The In1 GND plane survived remarkably well (one connected sheet, no sub-1mm bottleneck anywhere except one severed 2mm² fragment), but the In2 3V3 disc did not — it is carved into lobes that hang on 0.1–0.7mm isthmuses. That, plus re-stitching a few orphaned F.Cu pour pieces, is where I'd spend the evening. Items 8+ are aesthetics only.

P11. 3V3 disc (In2): the LDO feed lobe hangs on 0.3–0.7mm necks

U3's 3V3 output stitches into the In2 disc on the west lobe; that lobe reaches the rest of the disc only through the circled isthmuses between the PWR_EN channel and the VBATT/QSPI_SD2 via antipads: (94.8,102.4) 0.60mm, (94.0,103.3) 0.67mm, (93.4,104.4) 0.30mm, (92.5,104.8) 0.47mm. Every 3.3V electron for the MCU decap bank funnels through here.

Heals cheaply: slide the PWR_EN In2 run ~0.3–0.5mm west (it has open moat on its left flank the whole way), and nudge the VBATT + QSPI_SD2 vias near (94.7,102.3) apart so their antipads stop necklacing. Bonus: the dangling 3V3 via at (94.15,98.40) (red circle) sits in a clearance pocket doing nothing — shift it ~0.4mm into disc copper and it becomes a second feed into the lobe.

P12. 3V3 disc (In2): 0.10mm sliver — effectively a break

At (95.23,93.03) the disc's northwest arm connects through a 0.10mm sliver pinched between the VBUS In2 diagonal and a SWCLK via antipad. That's below anything the fab will reliably plate as a power path — treat the NW arm as severed today.

Nudge the VBUS diagonal ~0.3mm southwest (open moat below it) or the SWCLK via, and the arm reattaches with real width.

P13. In1 GND: the southeast channel — 4 nets wide, plus a severed fragment

QSPI_SD3, CHRG_DONE, QSPI_SD1 and VBATT share one ~2mm-wide diagonal channel through the GND plane below the MCU, then CHRG_DONE+VBATT continue east where a VBUS hook conspires with them to cut off a 2.0mm² plane fragment at (105.7,106.5) (alive only through 2 vias). Signals crossing above/below the channel lose their return: USB_DP 3.4mm bare run on B.Cu near (97.4,103.4), USB_DM 1.8mm at (97.8,104.4), QSPI_SD2 1.8mm on F.Cu at (101.4,106.2).

Can't be fully healed (those nets must reach east somehow), but it narrows: tuck CHRG_DONE tight against QSPI_SD1 so they share one clearance, and move the short VBUS In1 hook ((104.5,106.2)–(107.2,107.5), only 4mm of track) down to In2/B.Cu — that alone reattaches the fragment.

P24. F.Cu GND pour: two big crescents are barely stitched

The front pour is 8 pieces. The top crescent (59mm², whole USB/tip arc) hangs on 2 GND vias; the bottom crescent (51mm²) hangs on one. They're over solid In1 GND so nothing is floating, but as EMI skins / return stitches they're doing nothing, and a single-via 51mm² sheet is a resonator with a pin.

Sprinkle stitching vias — 4–6 per crescent near the LED ring drops (vias are free at JLC). The small 2–3mm² islands at (105.4,98.4), (105.3,100.6), (108.1,104.2), (94.4,105.2) each have ≥1 via already — one more each if you're in the neighborhood. The 11mm² ring under the QFN ((100,99.5)) is fine: it's grounded by the EP + 9 thermal vias.

P25. CHRG_DONE ties a literal knot at the charger

CHRG_DONE (routed 21.5mm vs 10.8mm point-to-point, 2.0×) wraps a full loop around the via cluster at (108.2,105.4), and CHRG (1.9×) crosses the same corridor twice on its way west. These are open-drain status lines — electrically they don't care — but the loop + double-cross costs ~8mm of In1 plane channel for nothing.

Classic "abstract puzzle" spot: unwind the yellow loop so it approaches its via from the west directly, and let VBATT's arrow-hook ((105.7,105.9)) drop straight instead. This is the same neighborhood as item 3's severed fragment — one session fixes both.

P26. IMU pocket: SDA/SCL knot, trapped sliver, dangling GND jumper

IMU_SDA (1.67×, 3 vias) does an S-hook around IMU_SCL at U6's north edge (upper circle), trapping a 1.1mm² back-pour sliver at (106.6,90.8). Nearby, an In2 GND jumper ends dangling at (106.16,93.64) — 0.34mm past its last connection (this is the one DRC also flags).

Re-approach SDA so it lands on its pad from the direction its In1 vertical already travels (no hook), which un-traps the sliver; trim the dangling GND tail.

P27. USB pair: gap breathes, and it crosses its own broken return

USB_DP/USB_DM ride together down the west corridor (good) but the intra-pair gap breathes between ~0.5 and ~1.0mm, and the final diagonal to the U1 vias crosses the item-3 In1 channel — DP's 3.4mm bare-return run is the longest on the board. FS-USB (12Mbps) is forgiving, so this is polish, not a bug.

Tighten the pair to a constant gap through the corridor; if you narrow the item-3 channel first, the bare-return run shrinks on its own. Cheap alternative: two GND stitching vias flanking the crossing so the return detour is short.

P38. USB_C fanout: DP's S-wrap under the connector

USB_C_DP goes pad → up to its duplicate-pad channel via → back down → wide S around the ESD chip; USB_C_DN elbows at (100.9,88.0). The trip up to the channel via is required (interleaved duplicate pads are joined there by design), but the S below is untangleable.

P39. SWD pocket west of the MCU: three staircases in one window

SWCLK hard-90s at (96.62,95.81) then runs stacked with RUN's own staircase; SWDIO zigzags below. Debug-only nets — pure aesthetics, but it's a satisfying 10-minute untangle.

P310. Crystal: XTAL_R staircase, XIN double-jog

XTAL_R steps 90°-45°-90° into R4 at (98.6,94.1); XIN has a redundant jog pair on its way to pin 21. Short, guarded, over solid GND — cosmetic only.

P311. VBUS takes the scenic route: 63mm for a 30mm job

VBUS runs J1 → west moat → around the bottom → charger, 2.1× the direct distance, ending in its own mini-switchback at U2. Riding the annulus/disc moat is actually clever (it displaces no plane copper), but the east moat reaches U2 in roughly half the length. ~40mA charge current → zero electrical urgency; listed because you asked for circuitous wiring, and this is the champion.

  • B.Cu pour slivers along the hand-routed corridors (17 sub-0.8mm necks; see full map below). The sheet stays connected; optionally add a via to the bigger tongues.
  • Dangling 3V3 vias (KiCad-flagged, freerouting leftovers): (94.15,98.4) — reuse for item 1! — plus (106.82,91.76), (106.2,102.6), (105.02,88.22), (101.09,105.6), (106.3,86.14). Delete or make them land in copper.
  • VBATT F.Cu tail at (94.17,102.54): track end sits ~0.15mm off its via center (hidden under the annulus — cosmetic in the file only).
